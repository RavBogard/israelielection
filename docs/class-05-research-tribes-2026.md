# Research memo — Israel's communities beyond Rivlin's four tribes (compiled 2026-10-04, Opus subagent)
Every fact ends (source, date, URL). [arith] = our arithmetic. Hebrew quotes are translations. CBS site blocked automated reading; CBS 2026 figures come via Israeli press reports of the Sept 9, 2026 release. Check quotes against originals before putting them on a slide.

## 1. Frameworks since Rivlin
1. Rivlin 2021: "We have gone from being a society in which a sizeable majority of Israelis are both Zionist and secular to one in which we have four main groups." (Moment, 2021-03-31, https://momentmag.com/reuven-rivlin-interview/). Nothing from Rivlin on the tribes after 2021 found.
2. Israeli Hope in Academia: 40 partner institutions, ~10,000 reached; newest figures 2017–18. (EdRF page, undated, https://www.edrf.org.il/en/programs/israeli-hope-in-academia/)
3. Rosner & Fuchs, #IsraeliJudaism (JPPI, 2019 survey n≈3,000): two axes (tradition × nationalism); "Jewraelis" 55%, "Israelis" 15%, "Jews" (mostly Haredi) 17%, "Universalists" 13%. (https://ij.jppi.org.il/english/summary) Pre-2022; no newer edition.
4. JPPI Israeli Society Index Aug 2024: "totally secular" 30% of Israeli Jews, "secular-traditional" 20%; 25% of secular-traditional say Oct 7 brought them closer to Judaism; 20% of totally secular say it distanced them. (JPPI 2024-08-15, https://jppi.org.il/en/18215-2/)
5. JPPI Nov 2025: 27% of Jews report more religious observance (33% of ≤25); "hard right" self-ID among Jews rose 11%→19%. (JPPI 2025-11-20)
6. JPPI Annual Assessment 2025 §8 identity narratives: "New Jew" (Israeli first, 36% Pew); ethno-religious nationalism (Jewish first, 45% Pew); Haredi; Religious Zionists "16–17% of the general population… 30 and 40%" of combat units. (JPPI 2025-09-15, https://jppi.org.il/en/annual-assessment-of-the-jewish-people-2025-5785/s/8/)
7. Masortim as the missing tribe: Even Tzur (JPPI 2023): leaving out masortim "a common mistake"; 19% use masorti as main identity, ~45% as secondary. (https://jppi.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/traditionalism_heb_2023-WEB-1.pdf). Danino (2021): "Traditionalists are the only hybrid tribe in Israel today."
8. "First Israel / Second Israel" (Avishai Ben Haim): secular Ashkenazi establishment vs Mizrahi traditional Likud-voting working class. (Jewish Currents n.d.; Israel Today 2020-09-14)
9. Goldman's "12 tribes" design project (c.2018): Haredi, Arab, Ashkenazi, Mizrahi, settlers, American Jews, Ethiopians, Russians, LGBTQ+, youth, elderly, refugees. Not academic.
10. Svetlova (2023-06-26) keeps four tribes; four school systems → four ideas of democracy. Sapir Journal 2025: 70% agree the division is "between people with centrist positions and the extremists" (Midgam Apr 2024).
11. IDI Democracy Index 2024 (fieldwork May 2024): right–left the most acute tension for 53% of Jews (43% in 2023); Jewish–Arab for 55.5% of Arabs. 2025 (fieldwork May 2025): right–left 55%; Jewish–Arab 54%. Trust 2025 Haredi vs secular: Supreme Court 3% vs 66%; government 45% vs 9%; IDF 61% vs 87%. 70% of Arabs vs 16% of Jews think Palestinian identity and loyalty to Israel can combine. (IDI Nov 2025, https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf)
12. "Jewish first": 57% of Israeli Jews, up from 34% a year earlier (Diaspora Affairs Ministry survey Feb 2026, n=1,216, via ToI 2026-06-02).
13. Pew 2025-03-26 switching study (2024 fieldwork): 22% of Israeli Jews switched category since childhood; Hilonim retain 93%; Datiim/Masortim ~60%; <1% of Haredim switch. Hiloni = 45%.
14. Not found: aChord taxonomy; JPPI Pluralism Index after 2021; Taub tribes framework; peer-reviewed 2023–26 taxonomy.

## 2. Community by community
a. Secular/hiloni: 39.6% of Jews 20+ (CBS Sept 2026 via Ynet 2026-09-09, https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/sjc9lpcufl), down from 42.7% (CBS Sept 2025). 2022 vote: Yesh Atid 39, Likud 20, National Unity 13, Labor+Meretz 16; coalition 26 / opposition 74 (IDI/Finkelstein 2026-06-28, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/64807). Most liberal cities: Hod Hasharon, Givatayim, Tel Aviv, Ramat Hasharon; Netivot last (Israel Hofsheet index via JPost 2025-12-10). Secular women heading to ~1.7 children (Taub Dec 2025).
b. Masortim: 21.8% "traditional not so religious" + 14.0% "traditional-religious" = 35.8% of Jews 20+ [arith] (CBS 2026). 2022 vote: Likud 46.5, RZ–Otzma 14, Yesh Atid 13, National Unity 12.5; coalition 66.5; Likud voters 57% masorti (IDI 2026-06-28). 87% of masortim: "Judaism is far more than observing commandments" (JPPI 2023). ~25% of Israelis live in development towns/southern cities; Likud 20–40% above national share there (ToI 2019-04-30 — old).
c. National-religious: 12.4% of Jews 20+ (CBS 2026); JPPI: 16–17% of general population. 2022 vote: RZ–Otzma 45 (from 30 in 2021), Likud 22.5, Shas 10.5, Jewish Home 9; coalition 80 (IDI 2026-06-28). Reserve service 2025, men serving in an average month: religious 4.3%, traditional 2.6%, secular 1.7%, Haredi 0.3% (IDI 2026-08-20, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/65427). Hardal size: not found.
d. Haredim: 1,452,350; 14.3%; +4.2%/yr; 57% under 19; male employment low 50s%; 3,060 enlisted 2024 (IDI 2025 via JPost Dec 2025). CBS 2026: 11.5% of Jews 20+. Streams: Lithuanian 433k (34%), Sephardi 408k (32%), Hasidic excl. Chabad 369k (29%), Chabad ~50k (4%); cities: Jerusalem 292,634 (28.9%), Bnei Brak 209,710 (94.8%), Beit Shemesh 108,054, Modiin Illit 86,601, Beitar Illit 67,147 (Machon Dec 2024, https://machon.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/2024-301224-he.pdf). 2022 vote: UTJ 58, Shas 28, RZ–Otzma 7, Likud 4 (IDI). Haredi first-time voters 2026: UTJ 45, Shas 20, Otzma 13 (N12 via JPost 2026-09-23).
e. Russian-speakers: ~1.3M (~15%) (Wikipedia 2022); ~1M eligible voters (~15%) (Lazar via JPost 2025-12-10, https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-879843). 2025 poll n=663: YB 23%, Bennett 17%; among 2022–25 arrivals Edelstein 37%; ~70% don't want Netanyahu to stay; 94% back universal draft. Apr 2019 YB got 40.2% of Russian-speaking vote (ToI 2019-07-26). Since 2022: ~80,000 from Russia, ~20,000 from Ukraine+Belarus (JPost 2025-09-20); Russia arrivals 43,500 (2022), 19,500 (2024), ~8,300 (2025). 71.7% of 2020 FSU immigrants not Jewish under halacha (Knesset RIC via ToI 2022-11-17). "Others" 4.7–4.9% of population; 29.6–33.7% of 2023–24 emigrants (Taub Dec 2025).
f. Ethiopian Israelis: 177,600 (2024): 93,400 Ethiopia-born, 84,200 Israel-born; ~2.3% of Jews; Netanya ~13,300 (CBS via JNS 2025-11-17). Voting study: not found.
g. Arab citizens: 2,173,000 (21.7%) (CBS 2026-09-09). Muslims ~1.849M (CBS via TPS 2026-05-26). Christians 184,200 (1.9%), 78.7% Arab (CBS via JPost Dec 2025). Druze 153.0k (2024, via Wikipedia); Golan Druze 29,364, ~20% naturalized by mid-2022 (Wikipedia). Negev Bedouin ~300,000, ~100,000 in ~35 unrecognized villages (IWGIA 2024). 2022 turnout 53.2%; Ra'am 35.2, Hadash–Ta'al 28.8, Balad 21.7, Zionist parties 14.3; Negev Bedouin Ra'am 73.9%; Druze ~90% Zionist parties (IDI/Rudnitzky 2022-12-25). May 2026 survey: turnout 53%, 67% if Joint List reunited; 77.2% back an Arab party joining the coalition (Dayan Center/KAP, https://dayan.org/content/integration-and-influence-survey-arab-citizens-israel). 252 Arab citizens murdered in 2025, a record (J Street 2026).
h. Settlers: West Bank 540,327 Jan 2026 (+2.01%), ~5.25% of population; Modiin Illit, Beitar Illit, Ma'ale Adumim, Ariel = 42.6% (Yesha via Sada News 2026-02-27). EU: 503,732 WB + 233,600 East Jerusalem end-2024 (EEAS 2025-08-14). Composition 36% Haredi / 36% RZ / 28% secular (Yesha via ToI 2023-05-12). 2022: RZ 22% in settlements vs 11% nationally; 59.7% east of barrier; settlers "only 5% of Israeli voters" (Washington Institute 2022-11-22).
i. Western olim 2025: US ~3,500, France ~3,300 (+45%), UK 840, Canada 420 (ToI); NBN 100,000th oleh 2026-08-09. Political lean: not found.
j. Reservists: Hendel's party launched 2025-09-18, "between the blocs"; up to 450,000 call-ups authorized May 2025; avg 136 reserve days/yr; 41% left or lost jobs (ToI 2025-05-30). Reservists' politics survey: not found. Youth: 74% of first-time Jewish voters right/center-right (N12 2026-09-23).

## Disagreements and gaps
ToI misprinted the 2026 religiosity (adds to 104%); use 39.6. Secular 39.6 (CBS 2026) vs 45 (Pew 2024). National-religious 12.4% of Jewish adults (CBS) vs 16–17% of population (JPPI). Haredim 14.3% all ages (IDI) vs 11.5% of Jewish adults (CBS) vs 15% (Taub ISS 2024). Masortim 35.8 vs 19 (main identity). Russian-speakers 1.3M vs ~1M voters. Emigration CBS vs TAU differ 10–20k/yr. Settlers Yesha 540k vs EU 504k. Not found: first grade 2026–27; CBS origin after 2015; Ethiopian/Druze 2025 counts; CBS Bedouin count; modern-Haredi size; reservists' politics; Rivlin after 2021.
