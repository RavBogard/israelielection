# Research memo — Oct 6 session: the system, the tribes, the map
Compiled 2026-10-04 by Opus subagent from web sources; every fact carries (source, date, URL). "[arith]" = our own arithmetic from sourced figures. Haaretz blocks automated reading; only headlines seen.

## A. Mechanics
1. 120 seats; elections "general, national, direct, equal, secret and proportional" (Basic Law: The Knesset ss.3–4; changeable only by majority of all MKs). (Constitute Project, Israel 1958 rev. 2013, https://www.constituteproject.org/constitution/Israel_2013)
2. One national district, closed lists. (Wikipedia, 2026 Israeli legislative election, acc. 2026-10-04, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)
3. Knesset may extend its own term only with 80 votes (s.9A). (Constitute)
4. A list may be barred for denying Israel as "a Jewish and democratic state," incitement to racism, or support for armed struggle (s.7A); Supreme Court must confirm. (Constitute)
5–7. Threshold history — SOURCES DISAGREE: IDI/Kenig 2015-01-26: 1% through 1988, 1.5% 1992–2003, 2% 2004–2013, 3.25% from 2014 (https://en.idi.org.il/articles/5225). IDI/Shapira 2022-11-08: 0.83% 1949, 1% 1951, 1.5% 1992, 2% 2006, 3.25% 2015 (https://en.idi.org.il/articles/46256). Reconciliation: law passed 11 Mar 2014, first applied 2015 (Wikipedia Elections in Israel; Haaretz 2014-03-11).
8. For/against raising: reduce incentive for small parties vs. reduce Arab representation. (IDI/Kenig 2015)
9. 2026: ~7.56M eligible voters; ~172,000 votes to pass threshold. (IsraelEd 2026-09-28, https://israeled.org/38-party-lists-israels-2026-parliamentary-election/)
10. Seat allocation: discard sub-threshold votes; divide remaining by 120 for quota; full quotas → seats; remainder by D'Hondt (Bader-Ofer). (IDI/Kenig 2026-03-16, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/63750). Surplus-vote agreements: two lists treated as one for remainders; only if both pass threshold. (IDI Shapira & Rahat 2026-04-27, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/64020)
11. 2026 surplus pairs: Yashar–Democrats; B'Yachad–Yisrael Beiteinu. (Israel Policy Forum 120 Project, upd. 2026-09-24, https://israelpolicyforum.org/onetwenty/)
12. Wasted votes 2022: ~8.7% of votes, record. Meretz 150,793 (3.16%), Balad 138,617 (2.91%), Jewish Home 56,775 (1.19%) of 4,764,742 valid. (IDI 2022-11-08; Wikipedia 2022 election)
13. Apr 2019: New Right 138,598 (3.22%), Zehut 118,031 (2.74%) of 4,309,270. (Wikipedia Apr 2019 election; IDI 2026-04-27 ">quarter million wasted")
14. 2015: Yachad 125,158 (2.97%) missed. (Wikipedia 2015 election)
15–18. Government formation (Basic Law: The Government 2001 ss.7–11, https://aceproject.org/ero-en/regions/mideast/IL/israel-basic-law-the-government-2001): President consults within 7 days of results, assigns mandate; 28 days + up to 14; second candidate 28 days; then 61 MKs may name someone within 21 days → 14 days; else dissolution, election within 90 days.
19. Historically 20–100 days; no government sworn in under 50 days in two decades. (IDI/Kenig 2026-04-15, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/63982)
20. Confidence vote; 61 in practice. (IDI 2026-04-15, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/63976)
21. 2026 dates: diplomats vote Oct 20; election Oct 27; results certified Nov 4 (Wikipedia); mandate assigned by Nov 11 (IsraelEd 2026-09-28).
22. Voters 18+, candidates 21+. (Constitute)
23. No voting abroad except diplomats/official emissaries incl. Jewish Agency shlichim (IDI Kenig & Plesner 2020-02-18, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/29786); ships' crews; missions open 12 days early (Wikipedia Elections in Israel).
24. Double envelopes: soldiers (stations open 72h early), hospitals, prisons, disabled, diplomats; counted after election night. (Wikipedia; ToI 2019-09-18)
25. 2026: Likud trying to block campaign flying expats home to vote. (CNN 2026-09-19, headline only, https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/19/middleeast/israel-election-netanyahu-expatriates-intl)
26. East Jerusalem: ~350,000 permanent residents, not citizens; may vote municipally, not for Knesset. (Inter-Agency Task Force brief, upd. Dec 2022, https://www.acitaskforce.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Arab-Palestinian-Citizenship-and-Residency-2025.pdf)
27. Golan Druze: majority of ~24,000 residency-only (same brief); older: ~18,000 residents, ~700 citizens (+972, 2012-10-30).
28. Settlers vote from within the territories (Wikipedia); emergency regulations treat West Bank Israelis as residents for various laws (IDI/Libman 2022-06-07, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/39012). Specific polling-station statute: not found.
29. Election day is a national holiday. (Wikipedia)
30. Turnout overall / Arab sector: 2015 72.34% / 63.5% (Dayan Center; or 52% by another calc); Apr 2019 68.46% / 49.2%; Sep 2019 69.83% / 59.2%; 2020 71.52% / 64.8%; 2021 67.44% / 44.6%; 2022 70.63% / 53.2%. Overall: Wikipedia per-election articles. Arab: IDI Rudnitzky 2020-03-10 https://en.idi.org.il/articles/30961; 2019-09-23 https://en.idi.org.il/articles/28660; 2021-04-27 https://en.idi.org.il/articles/34420; 2022-12-25 https://en.idi.org.il/articles/47986; 2015 Dayan Center 2019-05-20 https://dayan.org/content/arab-voting-21st-knesset-elections.
31–36. Five elections: Apr 9 2019 → Lieberman refuses over draft law → Knesset dissolves itself May 30 2019 (first time before a government formed). Sep 17 2019 → deadlock, 21-day period ends Dec 11 2019. Mar 2 2020 → Netanyahu–Gantz rotation Apr 20, sworn May 17 2020; dissolved Dec 23 2020 over budget (RTÉ 2020-12-22). Mar 23 2021 → Bennett–Lapid sworn Jun 13 2021; dissolved Jun 30 2022 (Axios 2022-06-30). Nov 1 2022 → Netanyahu bloc 64 (ToI 2022-11-07, https://www.timesofisrael.com/how-netanyahu-and-his-allies-won-by-a-knockout-the-data/). 25th Knesset served full term, first since 1988; dissolved 17 Jul 2026 (Wikipedia; JPost 2026-07-13).

## B. The tribes
1. Rivlin, "Israeli Hope: Towards a New Israeli Order," 15th Herzliya Conference, 7 June 2015 (JPS dates it 9 June). English text: https://www.runi.ac.il/media/vtylyqpp/presidentspeech2015.pdf
2. Verbatim: "Israeli society is undergoing a far-reaching transformation." / "In the 1990s (as can be seen in the slide behind me), Israeli society comprised a clear and firm majority, with minority groups alongside it." / "Today, the first grade classes are composed of about 38% secular Jews, about 15% national religious, about one quarter Arabs, and close to a quarter Haredim." / "A reality in which there is no longer a clear majority, nor clear minority groups… four population sectors, or, if you will, four principal 'tribes', essentially different from each other, and growing closer in size." / "…created a 'new Israeli order'." / "…abandon the accepted view of a majority and minorities, and move to a new concept of partnership…" / four pillars: "a sense of security for each sector," "shared responsibility," "equity and equality," "a shared Israeli character – a shared 'Israeliness'." / "Only in this way, together and in partnership, shall we be able to rekindle the Israeli hope." / "The 'new Israeli order' is not an apocalyptic prophecy. It is the reality." (ToI 2015-06-07, https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-divided-along-tribal-lines-rivlin-warns/)
