# Party profiles: Yashar, B'Yachad, The Democrats, Yisrael Beiteinu (researched 2026-10-04, Opus subagent)
Hebrew quotes translated; quotes seen via summarizing fetch — check wording before slide use. Haaretz/Israel Hayom headlines only. "Wikipedia map" = Wikipedia only.

## YASHAR! (Eisenkot)
A. Eisenkot elected 2022 on Gantz's National Unity list; war cabinet observer Oct 2023; left government June 2024; resigned Knesset July 2, 2025. Founded "Yashar! with Eisenkot" Sept 16, 2025 with ~120 co-founders incl. Matan Kahana, Yoav Horowitz, Maj. Gen. (res.) Tal Russo, Wix president Nir Zohar, Shir Siegel (daughter of freed hostages) (ToI 2025-09-16, https://www.timesofisrael.com/gadi-eisenkot-founds-new-party-two-months-after-resigning-from-knesset/). Name from freed hostage Eli Sharabi: bringing captives home was "not about right or left, but straight (yashar)." Launch: "The party will place Israel's security and national interests above all other considerations… to ensure the State of Israel's existence as Jewish, democratic, and strong, in the spirit of the Declaration of Independence." 2026 joiners: Shaul Meridor (Apr 28), Orit Farkash-Hacohen (Apr 30), Yoram Cohen (May 5), Kamil Abu Rokon (Jul 22), Elazar Stern and Roy Folkman (Aug 24), Adi Altschuler (Sep 6). New list, no seat history (National Unity 12 seats in 2022).
B. Eisenkot b. May 19, 1960 Tiberias to Moroccan immigrants; Golani from 1978, commanded the brigade 1997–98; Northern Command 2006–11; Chief of Staff Feb 2015–Jan 2019; son Gal killed in Gaza Dec 2023 at 25, nephew Maor Cohen Eisenkot killed the next day. JTA: would be Israel's first Mizrahi PM (JTA 2026-09-09). List Sept 7 (40 names; ToI liveblog): 1 Eisenkot; 2 Yoram Cohen (ex-Shin Bet chief); 3 Orit Farkash-Hacohen; 4 Adi Altschuler (designated education minister); 5 Matan Kahana; 6 Chili Tropper; 7 Shaul Meridor; 8 Tair Ifergan; 9 Oshrat Gani Gonen; 10 Shira Shapira; 11 honorary candidates incl. former hostage Ohad Ben Ami.
C. Targets "Route 4" commuter suburbs — Petah Tikva, Kiryat Ono, Holon (ToI/Schneider 2026-07-01); courting former Likud voters — candidates "resemble the Likud of old — before loyalty to Netanyahu" (ToI 2026-09-14); JC: "security-minded, centrist or moderate-right, secular or religiously traditional" (JC 2026-10-04). Shas says Eisenkot threatens its traditional Sephardi voters (ToI 2026-08-21). First-time voters 18–22: Yashar 16%, the largest share (N12/Midgam; JPost 2026-09-23).
D. Draft: "National Service for All"; deferments capped at "up to 3% of each annual cohort for outstanding individuals"; evaders face "broad revocation of civil and economic benefits" (Ynet 2026-09-16, https://www.ynetnews.com/article/sjph72wyzx). Eisenkot: "an annual cohort is approximately 70,000 people, so 3% is about 2,200.... Israel can permit that" (JPost 2026-09-24 article-909543); target ≥10,000 Haredi recruits per cycle (JPost 2026-08-30). Bennett called it "an insane mistake" (JPost 2026-08-07). Judiciary: repeal the Judicial Selection Committee composition law, replace via Basic Law; opposes an override clause; "clearer rules for judicial review through a Basic Law" (Ynet 2026-09-22 hkcjtk1cme); constitution "based on a combination of the principles of the Declaration of Independence" (IDI party page https://en.idi.org.il/israeli-elections-and-parties/parties/yashar/). War: opposes the Board of Peace framework as "leaving Hamas in power": "The State of Israel must not accept a reality in which Hamas survives, rearms, and waits" (JPost 2026-08-01); hostages as a war goal: "Netanyahu initially opposed including that objective, and I forced it upon him on the ninth day of the war"; inquiry: "A state commission of inquiry must be established that will examine the last 10 years, from when I started my position" (JPost 2026-09-24). West Bank: opposes a Palestinian state and "always has" (ToI 2026-09-26); would put the Civil Administration back under military coordination; Haaretz headline 2026-07-31: "Eisenkot Keeps Running Away From What Everyone Knows He Believes." Religion-state: JC: "a secular party, but not anti-religious"; supports civil and same-sex marriage, "live and let live" on Shabbat (characterization); Eisenkot: "I am not religious, but I grew up in a traditional family and have profound respect for Judaism and tradition" (JPost 2026-09-24). Economy: 15% raise for all conscripts, +50% for third-year combat soldiers; renter protections (JPost 2026-09-28); reserve duty capped at 50 days/yr, 150 over three years (Ynet 2026-06-30); IDI: "developing Israel's periphery and reducing social gaps." Netanyahu: "pathological liar, in Hebrew and English"; Qatari money approval "between breach of trust and treason" (ToI 2026-09-26); pardon position not found. Arab parties: Abbas "won't be part of my next government"; "I do not see an Arab party that accepts my principles" (JPost 2026-09-24); JTA Sept 9 had said he "has not ruled out" Ra'am — stance hardened.
E. Slogan "Israel must win." Cyprus airport billboards: "Landed? Had fun? Now come back and win!" (Ynet ~2026-07-27). Aug 14–17 floated a 52–58 seat minority government; Lieberman refused (ToI 2026-08-17). Sept 17: "Rotation is not on the table" (INN). Oct 4: written debate challenge to Netanyahu (Kipa). Sept 26 meeting at Lapid's home — Eisenkot, Bennett, Lapid, Golan, Lieberman (not Gantz): "Despite ideological differences among us, the formation of a Zionist, statesmanlike government dedicated to repair and reconstruction is our shared overarching goal"; agreed not to attack each other, working groups within 48 hours; NO PM candidate or rotation agreed; Bennett and Lieberman declined to endorse Eisenkot (ToI 2026-09-26).
F. Partners must accept four principles: Jewish democratic state; Declaration of Independence values; obligation of service; Hamas a terror group "to be dismantled and destroyed." "I do not rule out any Zionist party." No Haredi party currently meets his criteria. Surplus deal with the Democrats signed Sept 10 (ToI).
G. Seats: Maariv 21; Ch 13 21; Zman 21; Kan 23; Ch 14 22 (Sept 23) / 21 (Sept 28, Oct 1).
H. Named after Eli Sharabi's "not about right or left, but straight." JTA: "short and overweight," "often attending events in a simple black T-shirt and jeans."

## B'YACHAD / TOGETHER (Bennett, with Yesh Atid)
A. Bennett: Jewish Home chair 2012–18 (12 seats 2013); New Right Dec 2018, missed threshold Apr 2019 (138,598, 3.22%); Yamina 7 (Sep 2019), 6 (2020), 7 (2021); PM June 13, 2021–June 30, 2022; retired June 29, 2022; registered Bennett 2026 on Apr 1, 2025; PM candidacy Nov 11, 2025. Yesh Atid: founded 2012; 19 seats 2013 (Lapid finance minister); 11 in 2015; inside Blue and White 2019–20; 17 in 2021 (anchored Bennett–Lapid govt); 24 in 2022, led opposition. Deal signed night of Apr 25, announced Apr 26, 2026: "Together – Led by Bennett," Bennett PM candidate; Lapid: "setting ego aside and doing what is right" (ToI 2026-04-26; JPost article-894194). Yesh Atid 10 of top 24 slots (Reshet Bet via Wikipedia; ToI says four of top ten). Not a formal merger.
B. Bennett b. Mar 25, 1972 Haifa to American immigrants; Sayeret Matkal, Maglan; co-founded Cyota (sold 2005, $145M); Netanyahu's chief of staff 2006–08; Yesha Council director 2010–12. List (ToI 2026-09-06): 1 Bennett; 2 Lapid; 3 Keren Terner (ex-DG finance & transport); 4 Merav Ben Ari (Yesh Atid MK); 5 Liran Avisar Ben-Horin (ex-DG Communications); 6 Noam Tibon (joined Yesh Atid last year); 7 Michal Negri (ex-Ra'anana DG); 8 Eitan Ginzburg (ex-Blue and White MK); 9 Meirav Cohen (Yesh Atid MK); 10 Yonatan Shalev (23-year-old reservist activist); 12 Ram Ben Barak; 14 Naor Shiri.
C. Yesh Atid 2022 voters 78.5% secular, 20% traditional; took 39% of secular vote, 13% of traditional (IDI 2026-06-28). 2022: Yesh Atid 847,435 votes, 17.79%, 24 seats; Bennett didn't run. Russian-speakers: Bennett 17% (Lazar, JPost 2025-12-10). First-time voters: 10% (N12). Polling fell from ~25 seats in May to roughly half (ToI 2026-09-07, https://www.timesofisrael.com/hoping-to-put-wind-back-in-sails-byachad-tacks-toward-liberal-zionist-center/).
D. Draft: "Every citizen… will enlist in the IDF or perform national civilian service… without exemptions"; end stipends for married yeshiva students (Ynet 2026-09-16); Bennett: "Bibi is talking about a 50% exemption, Gadi is talking about a 30% exemption, I am talking about 0 exemption" (JPost 2026-08-30). Judiciary: Basic Law on how Basic Laws are enacted and when the court may intervene; repeal judicial appointments law (would "politicize the court system") (Ynet 2026-09-22); constitution "in the spirit of the Declaration of Independence"; 8-year PM term limits. War: "We must not budge a centimeter from the red line until Hamas is disarmed" (JPost 2026-08-01); supports state commission on Oct 7. West Bank: "Anyone who opposes a Palestinian state should support settlement in E1"; opposes a state "under any constellation" (JPost 2026-08-30); dismantle illegal outposts while backing "legal settlement in Area C": "I'm a right-wing guy, I'm not changing my opinions. But I'm not a schmuck" (ToI 2026-06-23); Haaretz headline 2026-06-30: "Bennett Is Committed to Annexing the West Bank." Religion-state: civil marriage; local choice on Shabbat transit — "We should allow cities to choose" (JPost 2026-04-20); civil-partnership registry and Basic Law: Equality: "we will work for full equality of rights to protect the LGBTQ community" (Ynet 2026-09-14); Lapid: parallel civil marriage within 100 days (JPost 2026-09-15); IsraelEd: break Rabbinate's authority over marriage, conversion, kashrut, Shabbat. Economy: "We will cut food prices by 30% within three years… wage all-out war to break up the food cartels" (be-yahad.org.il). Pardon: Bennett: "I will support a binding arrangement that includes a respectful retirement from political life alongside the end of the trial" (ToI 2025-11-30); Lapid: "The president cannot pardon Netanyahu without accountability." Arab parties: will "only rely on Zionist parties" — explicit departure from his 2021 govt with Ra'am (ToI 2026-04-26).
E. Site slogan: "Fixing what's broken in Israel begins on October 27th." Sept 6 launch: "Israel is not being governed… we need the country's very best." Ynet: mostly positive campaign on security, cost of living, education; "parliaments" in Likud strongholds. Repositioned toward liberal-Zionist center (ToI 2026-09-07). Sept 29: "mass draft evasion and IDF's collapse" is "the worst danger" (ToI liveblog).
F. Zionist parties only; no Arab or Haredi parties; "no compromises on draft issues" (ToI 2026-05-27). Surplus deal with Yisrael Beiteinu signed Sept 10.
G. Seats: Maariv 13; Ch 13 11; Zman 10; Kan 11; Ch 14 7 (Sept 23) / 9 (Sept 28) / 8 (Oct 1).
H. "I'm a right-wing guy… But I'm not a schmuck." In 2015 opposed same-sex marriage; in 2026 pledges "full equality of rights."

## THE DEMOCRATS (Golan)
A. Labor founded Jan 23, 1968 (Mapai, Ahdut HaAvoda, Rafi); peak 49 seats 1969; 4 in 2022; PMs Meir, Rabin, Peres. Meretz formed Mar 1992 (Ratz, Mapam, Shinui); 12 seats 1992; 0 in 2022, first time ever. Golan won Labor leadership May 28, 2024 (95%); merger announced June 30, 2024, approved by delegates July 12, 2024; members ratified May 31, 2026. IDI: partial merger, both keep "organizational and institutional independence." Protest leaders joined 2026: Moshe Radman, Gaby Lasky, Omri Ronen, Moran Zer Katzenstein, Nava Rozolyo.
B. Golan b. May 14, 1962 Rishon LeZion; paratrooper; commanded Nahal; Home Front Command 2008–11; Northern Command 2011; deputy CoS Dec 2014–May 2017; politics 2019 (Democratic Union), Meretz, deputy economy minister; re-enlisted himself Oct 7 and rescued Nova survivors. List (June 20 primary, ~97,000 voted): 1 Golan; 2 Naama Lazimi; 3 Gilad Kariv; 4 Efrat Rayten; 5 Yaya Fink; 6 Gaby Lasky; 7 Omri Ronen; 8 Michal Rozin; 9 Moshe Radman; 10 Somaya Bashir; 12 Moran Zer Katzenstein; 14 Emilie Moatti; 20 Eran Etzion (slots 6, 8, 14 reserved for Meretz).
C. Labor+Meretz took 16% of secular vote 2022 (from 19%) (IDI). 2022: Labor 175,992 (3.69%, 4 seats); Meretz 150,793 (3.16%, 0). First-time voters 7%. Claims >100,000 members.
D. Draft: universal service "within a national and equal framework," benefits shifted to those who serve (Ynet 2026-09-16); Golan called mass Haredi conscription "impractical" — "people who lack education, lack motivation and lack physical capability"; Ben-Gvir sought his disqualification; spokesperson: supports universal service as "a process" (ToI 2026-08-05). Judiciary: urgent problems are "delays, overloaded courts and a shortage of judges"; intermediate appeals court (Ynet 2026-09-22); opposes 2023 reform; defends AG (JPost 2026-10-01 article-910100); "In our government, there will be zero criminal defendants. Zero" (JPost 2026-07-21). War: "topple the Hamas regime" (IDI). West Bank: opposes annexation and "escalating Jewish terror"; defund outposts; Civil Administration back to IDF (JPost 2026-10-01); Golan: expecting Israelis "to make the galactic leap from the current situation to a two-state solution is impossible"; proposes civil separation with Israeli security control (CSMonitor 2026-08-12); IPF: "Golan openly supports a two-state outcome." Religion-state: limited public transport on Shabbat in secular localities; civil marriage; amend Nation-State Law (IDI). Economy: "Reduce living costs, improve social services, rebuild the public sector"; JPost: detail "less clearly defined." Pardon: "only the guilty seek a pardon" (Tribune India 2025-11-30). Arab parties: "We believe that an Arab party can be part of the government. It is natural, right, appropriate and democratic" (Kan via ToI 2026-09-27) — party clarified this is its platform, not the bloc's consensus.
E. Slogans: "אפשר לסמוך על הדמוקרטים" / "You can rely on the Democrats" (won't join Netanyahu); site: "Without the Democrats there will be no democracy." May 27: "I am not ruling out the Haredim," then same day "the Haredim will be outside… that is a guarantee" (ToI). Sept 27 Arab-party remark used by Likud's campaign.
F. No Likud, no Haredi parties; open to Ra'am (Lieberman replied Oct 3: "There will be no coalition with the Arab parties"). Surplus deal with Yashar Sept 10.
G. Seats: Maariv 10; Ch 13 11; Zman 8; Kan 9; Ch 14 9.
H. "Zero criminal defendants. Zero." "Jewish-Arab partnership in government is a positive thing. That is absolutely our future." (ToI 2026-10-03)

## YISRAEL BEITEINU (Lieberman)
A. Founded 1999; first targeted Soviet immigrants, later "right-wing secular voters and… immigrants from the former Soviet Union"; peak 15 seats 2009 (IDI party page). Seats: 1999 4; 2003 3 (w/ National Union); 2006 11; 2009 15; 2013 13 (joint with Likud); 2015 6; Apr 2019 5; Sep 2019 8; 2020 7; 2021 7; 2022 6. Apr 2019 refusal over the draft law set off five elections. Apr 2019: 40.2% of Russian-speaking vote. 2026 list reaches beyond Russian-speakers.
B. Lieberman b. July 5, 1958 Chișinău; immigrated June 18, 1978; bouncer at HU's "Shablul" club; Likud DG 1993–96; PMO DG 1996–97; lives in Nokdim since 1988; FM 2009–12, 2013–15; defense minister 2016–18 (resigned Nov 14, 2018); finance minister 2021–22. List (JPost/ToI 2026-09-06): 1 Lieberman; 2 Rafi Ben Shitrit (ex-Beit She'an mayor); 3 Talya Lankri; 4 Oded Forer; 5 Yulia Malinovsky; 6 Sharon Sharabi (brother of Eli Sharabi); 7 Hamad Amar; 8 Evgeny Sova; 9 Elvira Kolikhman (Lod deputy mayor); 10 Dan Illouz (ex-Likud MK).
C. Russian-speakers (Lazar n=663, JPost 2025-12-10): YB 23%, Bennett 17%; among 2022–25 arrivals Edelstein 37%, Lieberman 32%; 94% back universal draft; ~70% don't want Netanyahu; 68% name cost of living top concern. 2022: 213,687 votes, 4.48%, 6 seats. First-time voters 6%.
D. Draft: everyone at 18, "Jews, Muslims, Christians, Druze and Circassians"; sanctions incl. travel bans, benefit denial (Ynet 2026-09-16); "The draft law will be the first we advance" (Ynet 2025-12-07). Judiciary: "a written constitution that would be put to the public in a national referendum," amendments need 80 MKs (Ynet 2026-09-22); IDI: presidential system. War: Board of Peace arrangements "a capitulation to terror, and a return to the policy of containment" (JPost 2026-08-12); state commission on Oct 7 a condition (ToI 2026-08-17). West Bank: "The Palestinian Authority must be dismantled" (JPost 2026-09-28); IDI: long-standing "population exchanges and the creation of two ethnically homogeneous states." Religion-state five-point pledge (Ynet 2025-12-07): civil marriage and divorce; Western Wall compromise; local choice on Shabbat transit ("Tel Aviv will not impose Shabbat transportation on Bnei Brak…"); mandatory core curriculum; repeal of laws favorable to Haredim. Economy: "right-wing fiscal positions" (IDI). Pardon: "You made a deal — a state in exchange for a pardon" (Ynet 2026-02-23); in 2019 backed a pardon if Netanyahu left politics. Arab parties: "no place for non-Zionist elements… not for the Arab parties and not for the haredi parties" (JPost 2025-09-21; repeated Oct 3, 2026).
E. Slogans: "אנחנו הליכוד של פעם" / "We are the Likud of yesteryear" (Ynet 2026-09-27); "Strong on security, strong on economy" (Likud used the same line in 2009 — C14 2026-08-24). Launch: Israel "cannot become a federation of religious and ethnic minorities"; wants a "Zionist, nationalist, and liberal government" (JPost 2026-09-06). Aug 17: "will not be a partner in any constellation of minority government, period." Sept 22 with Eisenkot: "What matters is the bloc." May 31: goal is to become PM.
F. No Netanyahu, no Arab parties, no Haredi parties. Conditions: Oct 7 inquiry, universal service, PM term limits, core curriculum, constitution by referendum (ToI 2026-08-17). Surplus deal with B'Yachad Sept 10.
G. Seats: Maariv 9; Ch 13 9; Zman 10; Kan 9; Ch 14 7/6/6.
H. "We are the Likud of yesteryear." The man who started the five-election cycle by refusing Netanyahu in 2019 now has Eli Sharabi's brother at no. 6.

## Disagreements and gaps
Yashar: Ra'am stance hardened Sept 9→26; Palestinian state (ToI/JTA opposes; JC "largely irrelevant"); pardon position, annexation, religiosity breakdown not found. B'Yachad: annexation (Haaretz headline vs June quotes); slot shares 10/24 vs 4/10; sides of Terner/Negri/Shalev; 2026 hostage position; Jewish Home 2015 seats. Democrats: two states (IPF vs CSMonitor); merger dates; platform PDF blocked; no specific plan to reverse 2023–25 judicial laws; Gaza plan; geography/age. YB: Palestinians (IsraelEd "two-state framework" vs dismantle PA / population exchanges); list length 19 vs 25; pardon reversal 2019→2026; no newer Russian-speakers poll; Lankri background. No public rotation/PM agreement in the opposition.
