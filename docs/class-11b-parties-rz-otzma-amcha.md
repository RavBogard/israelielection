# Party profiles: Religious Zionism–Zehut, Otzma Yehudit, Amcha Yisrael (researched 2026-10-04, Opus subagent)
"[Wiki-map]" = Wikipedia only. "[arith]" = our arithmetic.

## RELIGIOUS ZIONISM–ZEHUT (Smotrich, with Feiglin)
A. Lineage: Tkuma split from NRP 1998 (Hanan Porat, Zvi Hendel) → National Union 1999 [Wiki-map]. Smotrich chair since 2019. 2021 joint list (RZ+Otzma+Noam) 225,641 votes, 5.12%, 6 seats. 2022 joint list 516,470 votes, 10.84%, 14 seats; split Nov 20, 2022: RZ 7, Otzma 6, Noam 1. Minister Ofir Sofer and MK Moshe Solomon left over the Haredi draft exemption (Ynet 2026-07-26). Smotrich refused a renewed joint run with Ben-Gvir: "the Religious Zionist public can and should run on its own as a big, strong, significant party" (JPost 2026-08-13/14 article-905489). Sept 1, 2026: "technical bloc" with Feiglin's Zehut, separable after the election (JPost 2026-09-01 article-907259); Feiglin: "We are a totally separate party" (Israel365 2026-09-11). Zehut: founded 2015; 2.7% Apr 2019. Netanyahu: the merger showed "national responsibility" (JNS 2026-09-02).
B. Smotrich b. Feb 27, 1980 Haspin (Golan); lives Kedumim; lawyer (Ono); held three weeks by Shin Bet during 2005 disengagement protests, not charged; co-founded Regavim; MK 2015 (Jewish Home); Transport Minister 2019–20; Finance Minister since 2022; minister in Defense Ministry over West Bank civil affairs since 2023 [Wiki-map]. List: 1 Smotrich; 2 Feiglin; 3 Orit Strock; 4 Simcha Rothman; 5 Tzvika Mor; 6/7 Zvi Sukkot (sources differ); 8 Rabbi Yitzhak Zaga; 10 Omer Patziniach Waldman; 12 Ohad Tal (moved down: "Despite the personal cost, I fully support party chairman Minister Smotrich"); new: Reut Ben Haim (Tzav 9 founder), Lt. Col. (res.) Itamar Eitam (INN 2026-09-09). Zehut got slots 2, 8, 10, 11. Feiglin b. July 31, 1962 Haifa; Zo Artzeinu 1993; sedition conviction 1997; Likud MK 2013–15.
C. 2022 joint list: 45% of national-religious voters (from 30% in 2021), 14% of traditional, 7% of Haredim; list's voters: national-religious 43%, traditional 37%, secular 14%, Haredi 6% (IDI 2026-06-28). Settlements Nov 2022: 22% vs ~11% nationally; 59.7% east of barrier, 22.4% west; settlers <3.5% of eligible voters but 6% of RZP's votes (Washington Institute 2022-11-22). Finkelstein: Smotrich draws "mainstream Religious Zionists," Ben-Gvir "traditional Mizrahi voters from the periphery" (ToI 2026-04-15). Settler anger over the draft: "I don't have a political home today"; Smotrich "sold this country to the ultra-Orthodox" (ToI 2026-08-07).
D. Draft: July 14, 2026 law banning arrests of Haredi draft evaders passed 58–54; most RZ MKs for, Solomon against (ToI 2026-07-14). Smotrich: "arrests don't bring ultra-Orthodox to the army… they just make Jews fight one another"; needs "a process and consensus" (ToI 2026-08-07); also "haredim should be enlisted in the IDF" (JPost 2026-08-18 article-905686); platform: "Basic Law for Service Members under the principle of 'first in equality'" (Ynet sjph72wyzx). Judiciary: "These elections concern the judiciary — nobody can claim ignorance" (Ynet 2026-09-15); plan: justices chosen by secret ballot of all judges; AG split in two; 61-vote override; limits on standing; supermajority + full panel to strike down a law. War/Gaza: "return to the whole of Gaza in a big way," military rule before sovereignty (JPost 2026-08-18); opposed Oct 2025 ceasefire/hostage deal but stayed in government (Media Line 2025-10-09). West Bank: E1 ~3,400 units approved Aug 20, 2025 — "practically erases the two-state delusion"; "Every settlement… is another nail in the coffin of this dangerous idea" (ToI 2025-08-20). Feb 8, 2026 security cabinet: land registries published; Jordanian-era ban on non-Muslim land purchase repealed; enforcement widened into Areas A/B; Hebron building permits moved to Israel's defense establishment (ToI 2026-02-08); Yesha Council: "among the most significant decisions… since its return to Judea and Samaria." 103 new settlements approved during his tenure; calls to "abolish the Oslo Accords" (JPost 2026-08-18). Religion-state: "Jewish identity in state institutions and education" (israel2026.co.il). Economy: "competition-based solutions"; Zehut: flat tax, vouchers, professional army, cannabis legalization. Pardon: "Netanyahu has been persecuted for years by a corrupt judicial system that fabricated political cases" (ToI 2025-11-30). Arab parties: rules out; said cooperation with Ra'am worse than Hamas attacks (JPost paraphrase 2026-08-18).
E. Slogan "כן, סמוטריץ'" / "Yes, Smotrich"; pride in "revolution in Judea and Samaria" (Ynet ~2026-09-27). AI video "משחררים את החסימה" showing Supreme Court President Amit, AG Baharav-Miara, Justice Barak-Eraz dragged through burning streets; Eisenkot: "disgraceful and violent" (Ynet 2026-09-15). Sanctions on Smotrich: UK, Australia, Canada, NZ, Norway June 10, 2025 ("Britain already tried once to prevent us from settling the cradle of our homeland, and we will not allow it to do so again," INN); Netherlands July 2025; Spain Sept 2025; Slovenia (reversed June 2026); Ireland June 5, 2026 (Al Jazeera); MEMO counts 10 countries by July 6, 2026. Called Winter's refusal to merge "an irresponsible gamble on the future of right-wing rule" (JPost 2026-09-07).
F. Netanyahu for PM; no Arab parties. Surplus partner: Likud (agreed Sept 8; unsigned Sept 15 per Channel 14).
G. Seats: Maariv 5; Ch 13 7; Zman 5; Kan 6; Ch 14 7. Range 5–7.
H. The E1 quote. Feiglin on Channel 12 (2024): "As Hitler said, 'I cannot live if one Jew is left,' we can't live here if one 'Islamo-nazi' remains in Gaza" (MEE 2024-06-16).

## OTZMA YEHUDIT (Ben-Gvir)
A. Kach: Kahane 1971; one seat 1984; barred 1988 as "manifestly racist"; outlawed 1994 after Goldstein massacre; US removed terror designation May 2022 [Wiki-map]. Otzma founded Nov 13, 2012 (Eldad, Ben-Ari) [Wiki-map]; IPF: "ideological continuation" of Kahane's movement; JPost: "rooted in Rabbi Meir Kahane's movement." Results: 2013 1.76%; 2015 in Yachad, below; Sept 2019 1.88%; 2020 0.42%; 2021 1 seat on RZ list; 2022 6 seats. Ben-Gvir leader since 2019. Left government Jan 19, 2025 over ceasefire; returned March 2025.
B. Ben-Gvir b. May 6, 1976 Mevaseret Zion to Iraqi-Jewish parents; lives Kiryat Arba/Hebron; former Kach youth coordinator; barred from IDF at 18; ≥8 convictions incl. 2007 incitement to racism and support for a terror organization; lawyer for Jewish extremists; MK 2021; National Security Minister since late 2022 [Wiki-map]. List filed Sept 7 (ToI): 1 Ben-Gvir; 2 Tally Gotliv (ex-Likud; Ben-Gvir: "a fighter against the deep state"); 3 Yitzhak Wasserlauf; 4 Amichai Eliyahu; 5 Limor Son Har-Melech; 6 Yitzhak Kroizer; 12 Yishai Fleisher (Hebron spokesman). No primaries.
C. Finkelstein: "traditional Mizrahi voters from the periphery." Mitchell Barak: "Ben Gvir is hardcore, and today everyone wants hardcore" (ToI 2026-04-15). Haredi first-time voters: Otzma 13% (N12). Courting disappointed Likud and Shas voters: "Anyone whose heart is on the right side of the political map is welcome" (JPost 2026-08-22).
D. Draft: supported July 14 arrest-ban law; platform: Haredi tracks in police/Border Police — "enter service as Haredi and leave it as Haredi" (Ynet). Judiciary: backed 2023 reforms; political control of appointments. Gaza: "Disengagement 710" (Sept 3, 2026): emigration ministry; 250,000 departures/yr; 1.11M in three years, ~1.86M in seven; NIS 10bn — "we must seize this historic opportunity to bring about a historic solution" (JPost article-907523); "This is a free choice; we are not forcing anyone to leave, but there is encouragement" (JTA 2026-09-06). Oct 2025 deal: "happy for the release of all hostages, but we will vote against releasing murderers" (Media Line). West Bank/Temple Mount: opposes Palestinian state and evacuating settlers; Tisha B'Av 2026: "Jews are praying and feel that they are the rightful owners of this place" (JPost 2026-07-23). Death penalty for terrorists law passed March 30, 2026 [Wiki-map]. Economy 2026: not found. Trial: "I hope President Trump will put an end to this farce" (ToI 2026-01-27); Eliyahu to Herzog: "The power of pardon must not be conditioned on anything" (Ynet 2026-03-25). Arab lists: Otzma petitioned Sept 16 to disqualify Abu Shehadeh under 7A citing his Oct 8, 2023 article (INN 2026-09-16). Petition by Democrats/Zulat to disqualify Otzma failed Sept 23/24 (19–10–1); AG called evidence "highly troubling" but below threshold; Ben-Gvir: remarks "directed at enemies or terrorists rather than Arabs as a group" (JPost 2026-09-24).
E. Slogan "מי שמאמין לא מפחד" ("He who believes does not fear"); claimed results: 1,000 standby squads, ~400,000 gun licenses, death penalty law, end of "prisoner summer camps" (C14). Moments: Temple Mount prayer July 23; Gotliv joins Sept 3; Disengagement 710 Sept 3; Haaretz Sept 7 headline on his election team; Sept 15 Otzma: "Netanyahu wants Eisenkot in the next government instead of Ben-Gvir" (JFeed); Haaretz Sept 17 gun-shaped keychains at a high-school mock election; Ynet: influence campaign "with more than 100 bots"; Oct 3: Sohlberg ruled Ben-Gvir's prison video ("I want to kill you. I want to put you to death," to Hamas prisoner Hassan Salameh) misused public assets — removal, NIS 20,000 costs; Ben-Gvir: "extremely puzzling" (JPost 2026-10-03). Sanctions: same five countries June 10, 2025 ("we survived Pharaoh, we will survive Keir Starmer as well"); France May 2026; Ireland June 5, 2026; Netherlands, Spain, Slovenia (reversed).
F. "Fully right-wing government"; no unity with centrists, no Arab parties; demands Defense or Justice; no Palestinian-state talks (JPost article-909348). Surplus partner: none found (refused Winter).
G. Seats: Maariv 9; Ch 13 8; Zman 6; Kan 7; Ch 14 7. Range 6–9.
H. The prison video and the committee's order. "We survived Pharaoh, we will survive Keir Starmer."

## AMCHA YISRAEL / PEOPLE OF ISRAEL (Ofer Winter)
A. Winter retired from IDF May 2024 after being passed over for major general; turned down a Likud slot. Billboards: "Soon you will have someone to vote for." Launched Aug 25, 2026 at Jerusalem's Shalva Center, ~300 people (Ynet 2026-08-25; JVL). Netanyahu (via i24): "I don't trust Winter. He will either waste right-wing votes or take right-wing votes and hand them to a government headed by Eisenkot." Refused Smotrich's merger despite pressure from rabbis (INN 2026-09-07); Winter: the public wants "new people, those who were not part of the events that led us to October 7" (JPost 2026-09-07).
B. Winter b. Feb 3, 1971 Rekhasim; IDF 1990–2024; commanded Duvdevan, Givati, 98th Division; lives Mitzpe Netofa; eight children; Bnei David (Eli) graduate; 2014 Givati letter: Hamas "defies, curses and reviles the God of Israel's armies"; on Oct 7 went to the Be'eri front on his own. List (INN 2026-09-07): 1 Winter; 2 Yoseph Haddad (b. 1985 Haifa; Arab Orthodox Christian; Golani volunteer wounded at Bint Jbeil 2006; founded "Together – Vouch for Each Other" 2018; Winter wants him as public diplomacy minister); 3 Netali Shem Tov (journalist); 4 Eran Ben-Ari; 5 Fleur Hassan-Nahoum (b. 1973 London; Jerusalem deputy mayor 2018–24; UAE–Israel Business Council co-founder; Likud member 2019–2026); 6 Lali Deri (bereaved mother); 7 Ronen Budnero (Finance Ministry economist); 8 Davidi Ben Zion (Samaria Regional Council deputy head).
C. New; targets younger voters, reservists, right-wingers who can't back the current government (JPost 2026-09-17 article-908996). Sector data: not found.
D. Draft (central): "We will not join a government that does not pass, before it is formed, a law that puts an end to draft evasion… Full stop." "No sector is above the law. Those who serve will receive." (JPost 2026-09-02 article-907373). "If we fail to significantly integrate ultra-Orthodox recruits into the military, I will have failed"; double combat soldiers' pay (Ynet 2026-10-04). Will still sit with Haredim: "I won't join until this issue is sorted out. I go to war on this" (ToI 2026-10-04). Judiciary: "judicial system reform," no details. Gaza: "There is one solution in Gaza: migration." "There will never be a Palestinian state here." (Ynet 2026-08-25); "I believe 80% of Gazans want to leave. We're the ones preventing them"; supports Jewish return to Gaza; doctrine "initiative, deception, decisive action, victory" vs "containment"; inquiry: "immediate establishment of a commission of inquiry from the moment we take office," not a state commission (Ynet 2026-10-04). Economy: double combat pay. Netanyahu: "Netanyahu is responsible"; "I don't work for Netanyahu" (JPost 2026-09-17); called him "a sucker" (ToI 2026-10-04); will still recommend him: "We're on the right… period." Arab citizens: "greater Arab citizen integration" (JVL); Haddad: "I despise these people… I'm here to oust them from the Knesset"; "I want to work for Shfaram and Nazareth, not for Gaza and Jenin" (JFeed 2026-09-14).
E. Slogans: "Less politics, more leadership"; "What was will not be" (JVL); "הכרעה" ("Decision") (Ynet). Launch: "We are here to bring the people of the national camp home"; closed with "Hineni He'ani Mima'as." Sept 18: Yashar's Meridor says Winter "opposed the maneuver in Gaza" (JPost). Sanctions: none.
F. Recommends only Netanyahu; wants Defense and double-digit seats; won't partner with Bennett or Eisenkot (Ynet 2026-10-04; conflicting JPost reports). Surplus partner: none (Likud chose RZ; Ben-Gvir refused).
G. Seats: Maariv 4; Ch 13 5; Zman 4; Kan 4; Ch 14 below threshold (1.6%). Range 0–5.
H. Closed his launch reciting "Hineni He'ani Mima'as," echoing his 2014 Givati message. His Arab Christian no. 2: "I'm here to oust them from the Knesset."

## Disagreements and gaps
RZ: Sukkot slot 6 vs 7; slot 5 name garbled in one source; Likud surplus deal signed vs unsigned; no 2026 "voluntary emigration" quote from Smotrich; no outpost-legalization figures. Otzma: founders; poll summaries 7–10; death-penalty date; no 2026 economic platform; no surplus partner. Amcha: Wikipedia launch year wrong (2024); list order differs across sources; Bennett/Lieberman stance conflicting; Kan poll date Sept 27 vs 28; hostages, annexation, pardon, religion-state not found. Channel 14 "Sept 27" figures are actually its Sept 23 or Sept 28 polls. No IDI June 2026 vote-intention-by-religiosity exists; only the 2022 vote analysis (published 2026-06-28).
