# Brief — What is not on the ballot (issue-not-on-ballot)

## Findings that carry the page
1. Only Ra'am and the Joint List support a Palestinian state. Likud declined Ynet's questionnaire; Yashar: "not on the agenda"; the Democrats say "separation."
2. Knesset rejected a Palestinian state 68–9 (July 17, 2024), even via negotiation; Gantz's National Unity MKs voted yes, only Arab-led parties no.
3. Pew 2026: 17% of Israelis (16% of Jews) think coexistence with a Palestinian state is possible (50% in 2013); 56% say it is not, a first.
4. Peace Index, Jews, Sept 2023 → Mar 2026: state 38% → 23%; annexation with limited rights 30% → 38%; 48% expect the status quo.
5. With "separation" on the menu (INSS June 2026), it leads among Jews (32%), level with annexation (31%).
6. Palestinian citizens back two states (71%, Peace Index Mar 2026, Arab subsample of only n=103) but vote on crime first (53.5%, IDI Sept 2026).
7. Jewish vote priorities (IDI, Sept 2026): security 39%, cost of living 38%, draft 27%; the Palestinian issue was not an option.
8. American Jews: 63% think coexistence possible (Pew 2020); 46% call two states the best outcome (Pew 2024).
