# Brief — Palestinian citizens of Israel

## Findings that carry the page
1. **Size depends on who is counted.** CBS (via ToI, Sept 2026): 2.17M Arabs, about 21% of 10.3M (ToI's printed 21.7% does not match its own total). This includes ~400,000 East Jerusalem residents, mostly non-citizens (IDI, end-2020 basis: ~362,000), and Golan Druze. Citizens only: ~1.74M, ~17% (our estimate). Druze sit inside every "Arab" figure.
2. **Turnout follows unity.** 64.8% when four parties ran together (2020); 44.6% split (2021); 53.2% in 2022.
3. **Ra'am holds the Negev; elsewhere a three-way split.** 2022: Ra'am 64% in Rahat. Balad's 138,617 votes fell below the threshold.
4. **Crime is the issue.** Killings: 116 (2022) → 245 → 230 → 252 (2025, record; report closed 28 Dec 2025 — the Abraham Initiatives homepage now gives 255 for the full year). 71–74% call crime the top issue; cost of living drives the vote (49%); Haredi draft 1.7%.
5. **They want in.** 77% back an Arab party in the coalition (KAP); 86% vs 27% of Jews (IDI).
6. **Identity answers depend on the question.** Single "most important component": Arab 33%, Israeli citizenship 28%, Palestinian 13.5% (May 2026). Compound labels: 62% chose a Palestinian label (Smooha 2019). CBS: Palestinian 3% (reported by JNS, May 2025; survey year and wording not stated).
7. **Wary, but not mainly an Oct 7 shift.** 70% say better not to voice views before strangers (IDI 2025); IDI says the big rise among Arabs came about a decade ago. "Feel part of the state" fell 66% → 54% (2024→2025), but IDI calls 2024 unusually high and says 2025 is still among the highest since tracking began in 2003.
