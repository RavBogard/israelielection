## 0. The name

**What the page should do (ruling already made):** lead with "Palestinian citizens of Israel," note "Arab Israelis," and explain the dispute.

**Who uses which term**
- Israeli government and the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) use "Arabs" / "Arab citizens" / "Arab Israelis." CBS counts "Arabs" as one population group that includes Muslims, Christian Arabs and Druze. [CBS via ToI 2026-09-09](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ahead-of-rosh-hashanah-israel-population-is-at-10305000-up-1-2-over-last-year/)
- The Israel Democracy Institute (IDI) and the Konrad Adenauer Program (KAP) at Tel Aviv University write "Arab citizens" / "Arab society" / "Arab Israelis." [IDI Democracy Index 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf); [KAP May 2026](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18837)
- The Abraham Initiatives (a Jewish-Arab shared-society NGO) uses "Arab citizens" in its crime report but "Palestinian citizens" on its homepage. [report](https://abrahaminitiatives.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2025-Crime-and-Violence-Report.pdf); [homepage](https://abrahaminitiatives.org/)
- Arab parties and Arab-led NGOs mostly use "Palestinian" or "Palestinian Arab"; BBC, NYT and Washington Post use "Palestinian citizens of Israel" or alternate. Other terms: "'48 Arabs," "Arabs of the Interior." (Wikipedia summary; secondhand.) [Wikipedia: Terms for Palestinian citizens of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terms_for_Palestinian_citizens_of_Israel)
- Mansour Abbas's self-description: "an Arab, a Palestinian, a Muslim, and a citizen of the State of Israel" (class doc 11d; not re-opened).

What is worth noticing:
- When the label offered is a compound ("Palestinian in Israel"), Palestinian wins (Smooha 2019: 47% + 15% = 62% chose a Palestinian label). When asked to pick one *component*, "Arab" leads and "Palestinian" alone is a minority (9–17%). When CBS asks primary identity, Palestinian is 3%.
- "Israeli citizenship" jumped after Oct 7 (21% → 33%) and has drifted back (27.7% in May 2026). KAP itself notes it offered "citizenship" (an instrumental tie) rather than "Israeli identity" (an emotional one). [KAP Nov 2025, p.19](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18732)
- Big differences by religion: Druze overwhelmingly put Israeli citizenship first (64.8%); Christians split between Israeli citizenship and Arab; Muslims put Arab first. By party: Balad voters put Palestinian first; Jewish-party voters put Israeli citizenship first. [KAP May 2026](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18837)
- Framing flag: JNS headlined the CBS data as "Most Arab Israelis do not consider Israeli identity primary"; KAP press framed similar data as Israeli citizenship becoming "one of the more important components." Both are accurate readings of different numbers.

## 1. Size, growth, geography

**Total, as CBS counts it:** 2,173,000 Arabs, 21.7% of 10,305,000 (CBS Rosh Hashanah release, Sept 9, 2026). [ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ahead-of-rosh-hashanah-israel-population-is-at-10305000-up-1-2-over-last-year/). CBS's "Arabs" includes East Jerusalem Palestinians (most of whom are permanent residents, not citizens) and Golan Druze. IDI states this directly for its 2020 base year: "This figure includes almost 362,000 Arab residents of East Jerusalem who hold 'permanent resident' status, but not full citizenship." [IDI Statistical Report on Arab Society 2021](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/38540)

**Excluding East Jerusalem and the Golan:**
- IDI, end-2020: 1,957,270 Arabs total (21.1%); excluding East Jerusalem 1,595,300 (17.2%). [IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/38540)
- 2026 estimate (my arithmetic, flag for Daniel): 2,173,000 minus ~408,000 East Jerusalem Arabs (CBS end-2025 Jerusalem 1.063M × 38.4% Arab, via class doc 06, secondhand) minus ~27,000–29,000 Golan Druze ≈ **1.74 million**, about 17% of the population. Imprecise in both directions: a minority of East Jerusalemites and about a third of Golan Druze now hold citizenship (Globes 2026-05-24 via class doc 06). The Inter-Agency Task Force brief still says "approx. 1.5 mil" (updated Dec 2022) — stale. [ITF](https://www.acitaskforce.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Arab-Palestinian-Citizenship-and-Residency-2025.pdf)
- Voter-roll check: in 2022 IDI analyzed Arab and Druze localities only; East Jerusalem residents mostly cannot vote for the Knesset (class doc 02).

**Religion (CBS figures via press; Druze page is another agent's):**
- Muslims 1.849 million (CBS, May 26, 2026). [TPS](https://tps.co.il/articles/israels-muslim-population-reaches-1-85-million-ahead-of-eid-al-adha/) (headline only visible). Includes East Jerusalem.
- Christians 184,200 (end-2024), of whom 78.7% are Arab, about 145,000 (CBS via JPost Dec 2025; class doc 06; verified: false).
- Druze about 152,000–153,000 (2022–2024; class docs; verified: false).
- Shares within CBS "Arabs" (arithmetic): Muslim ~85%, Christian Arab ~7%, Druze ~7%. IDI 2020 (citizens excl. East Jerusalem): Muslim 82.9%, Druze 9.2%, Christian 7.9%. [IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/38540)
- **Overlap note for the Druze page:** CBS, IDI's Arab sample, KAP's sample and IDI's vote analyses ("Arab and Druze localities") all include Druze inside "Arab." Most Druze reject the Arab label politically and vote ~90% for Zionist parties (class doc 05). Numbers on this page include Druze unless stated. The site should say so once, near the top.

**Regions (IDI, end-2020, citizens excl. East Jerusalem):** North/Galilee 51.6% (823,000); Triangle 19.7% (314,000); Negev 17.5% (280,000); mixed cities 8.3% (132,000); other 2.9%. [IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/38540) — stale (2020) but the only regional split found.

**Negev Bedouin:** about 325,000 at end-2023, about 20% of the Negev's population; grew sixfold 1995–2023; 48.6% under 17; 70,000–90,000 live in "dispersed" (unrecognized) communities (State Comptroller, reported Apr 28, 2026). [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/article-894474). IDI 2021: in the unrecognized villages, "home to around 70,000 residents, there are only 10 elementary schools, and not a single secondary school." [IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/38540). Number of unrecognized villages: ~35 (IWGIA 2024, class doc 05) vs "39–45" (Wikipedia) — not settled.

**Growth:** Muslim population grew 1.8% in 2025 (CBS via TPS, class doc 06), above the national 1.2%. Arab first-graders (Arab + Bedouin + Druze streams) were 22.9% of 2025–26 first grade, roughly level with their population share (class doc 02). Arab fertility has converged toward Jewish fertility — not re-verified this round.

**Income and education gaps (all older than two years — flag):**
- Poverty 2018: Arab families 45.3% vs Jewish 13.4%; Arab children 57.8% vs Jewish 21.2%. [IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/38540). National Insurance 2020 (via Wikipedia): Arab individuals 35.8% vs Jewish 17.7%. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty_in_Israel)
- Wages 2018: Arab men earn 77% of Jewish men; Arab women 61% of Jewish women. [IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/38540)
- Bachelor's degree: Arab citizens 10.3% (2007) → 13.6% (2019). Arab women's employment 19.8% → 38.2% (2001–2018). [IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/38540)
- Self-reported now: 73.4% of Arab respondents called their economic situation good in Nov 2025, the highest of the war-era KAP surveys. [KAP Nov 2025](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18732)

## 3. Attitudes on the six issues

The best cross-tab is IDI Democracy Index 2025 (fieldwork May 2025; Arab sample n=364, phone in Arabic, ±5.14). [IDI DI 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf)

**Which issue will most decide your vote (Q58), Arabs vs Jews:** cost of living 49.1% vs 16.8%; judicial overhaul 11.2% vs 16.4%; Netanyahu trial 10.7% vs 1.5%; Oct 7 7.1% vs 16.9%; hostages 6.5% vs 18.1%; Haredi conscription 1.7% vs 16.6%; protests 1.6% vs 2.5%. Factor (Q53): economy/cost of living platform 33% of Arabs vs 19% of Jews; religion-and-state platform 7.4% vs 19.4%. Crime platform a major factor: 72.4% of Arabs (41.7% very much). [IDI DI 2025 appendix pp.274–276](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf); [IDI 62794](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/62794)

By issue:
1. **Haredi draft:** almost no salience (1.7%). The adjacent question is Arab civic service: 75.8% support voluntary non-security civic service for Arab high-school graduates (69.1% of 18–34; Balad voters 51.9% opposed). [KAP May 2026](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18837). No survey found asking Arab citizens about Haredi conscription itself.
2. **Courts:** the Supreme Court is the institution Arabs trust most (40%; Jews 42%), but 55.5% of Arabs also say it "intervenes too much in decisions made by the government." 78% say a constitution is important (Jews 69%). [IDI DI 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf)
3. **War and hostages:** Dec 2023: 56% said the Oct 7 attack does not reflect Arab society, the Palestinian people or the Islamic nation; 33% said it does (n=538). [IDI 52016](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/52016). Trust in the IDF 2025: Muslims 28%, Christians 34%, Druze 84%. [IDI DI 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf). Nov 2025: 74.6% say the war damaged Arab-Jewish relations. [KAP Nov 2025](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18732)
4. **West Bank / Palestinian state:** most realistic solution: two states on 1967 lines 46.4%, no solution 21.4%, one state 18.7%, regional 7.1% (May 2026). Trend: two states 17.2% (May 2023, when 55.6% said no solution) → 49.7% (Dec 2024) → 47.3% (Nov 2025) → 46.4%. [KAP May 2026](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18837); [ToI Dec 2024](https://www.timesofisrael.com/survey-finds-arab-israelis-show-growing-sense-of-shared-destiny-since-outbreak-of-war/). Note that this asks what is "realistic," not what is preferred.
5. **Religion and state:** 80% of Arabs say the Jewish component of Israel's identity is "too dominant" (Jews 44%); 61% expect more "religionization." Nation-State Law (2018): 72.5% of Arabs in an IDI poll said equality should have been in it (Wikipedia, secondhand). [IDI DI 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Law:_Israel_as_the_Nation-State_of_the_Jewish_People)
6. **Economy:** top vote driver (above). The top agenda item overall is crime: 71.3% (May 2026), 74% (Nov 2025). [KAP](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18837)

**Coalition question:** 77.2% support an Arab party joining the next coalition (43.3% any coalition, 33.9% center-left only), stable since 2023 (63.3% May 2023). [KAP May 2026](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18837). IDI: 86% of Arabs vs 27% of Jews support bringing Arab parties into government (Jewish Right 11%). [IDI DI 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf)

**Belonging:** "feel part of the state and its problems" — Arabs 66% (2024) → 54% (2025); Muslims 52.5%, Christians 56.5%, Druze 68%. Proud to be Israeli: Arabs 44%, Jews 83%. Optimistic about Israel's future: Arabs 45%. [IDI DI 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf)

aChord: their identity tracking is reported only via Statista (paywalled); no aChord Arab-citizen survey was opened. Gap.

## 4. Lived texture

**Ra'am in government, 2021:** On June 2, 2021, Ra'am became the first independent Arab party to join an Israeli governing coalition (Bennett–Lapid); it has since separated from the Islamic Movement's Shura Council (declared complete July 2026) and opened membership to all Israelis (class doc 11d). Abbas on the 2026 choice: "The option of a joint list of four parties is off the table. There is discussion of a list comprising Hadash, Ta'al and Balad, while Ra'am will run independently." [Ynet 2026-08-02](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/s1xafktsfg)

**After Oct 7 — speech:** 70% of Arabs (vs about half of Jews) say it is better not to express political opinions in front of strangers; about three-quarters of Arabs say freedom of expression in Israel is more limited than in other democracies; only 16% say no one hesitates to speak (27% in 2016). [IDI DI 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf). Press accounts of arrests over posts: CNN (Nov 5, 2023) reported "dozens" arrested for expressions of solidarity with Gaza civilians; El País reported "hundreds" of interrogations (both via Wikipedia; not opened). JNS reports 170+ citizens detained for national-security questioning in 2024 and 26 cases of pledging allegiance to ISIS or other groups (secondhand; JNS does not give its source in the text read). A primary count (police or Adalah) was not found.

**Nation-State Law:** passed July 19, 2018, 62–55; "The right to exercise national self-determination in the State of Israel is unique to the Jewish people"; Arabic moved from official language to "special status"; upheld by the Supreme Court 10–1 in July 2021 (Justice George Karra the sole dissenter). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Law:_Israel_as_the_Nation-State_of_the_Jewish_People) (primary Knesset site unreachable).

**Young voters:** "It makes no difference who you vote for": Arabs 18–34 50%, 35–54 50%, 55+ 37% (Jews 40 / 43 / 31.5). [IDI DI 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf). Belief in Arab-Jewish political partnership among 18–34: 51.6% (Nov 2025) → 59.3% (May 2026); in Nov 2025, 33.6% of the young said they did not believe in it at all. [KAP](https://dayan.org/file-download/download/public/18837). Expected youth turnout ~59% (Inter-Agency Task Force 2026-08-12, class doc 11d, not opened). Half the 2025 murder victims were 18–30.
