## Must fix before the brief goes to Daniel
1. **Population headline:** ToI's 21.7% does not match its own total. Either quote CBS directly, or write "2.17M (about 21% of 10.3M)". Do not print 21.7% next to 10.3M.
2. **Chart C Nov 2023 column:** the cited PDF does not contain it. Find the KAP Nov 2023 report, or drop the column and say so.
3. **Attribution:** the May 2026 and Nov 2025 surveys are KAP (Konrad Adenauer Program, Dayan Center). Remove "Statnet" from the chart C label and from the "Statnet (May 2026)" sentence. Statnet is correct only for the 2022 forecast.
4. **Finding 7 ("After Oct 7, quiet"):** IDI itself says the speech-chill rise among Arabs happened about a decade ago, and that 2024's belonging figure was an outlier while 2025 is still among the highest on record. Reword so the page does not present either as an Oct 7 effect.
5. **2025 toll:** 252 is the report figure (closing date 28 Dec); the homepage says 255. Pick one and footnote it.
6. **2026 seat range:** cite a real poll source; Wikipedia's late-Aug range is wider (JL 5–10, Ra'am 4–6).
7. **Note on turnout:** IDI's "Arab" turnout covers Arab **and Druze** localities. Add this to the chart B note (it ties to framing item 3).
