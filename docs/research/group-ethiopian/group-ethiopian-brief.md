# Ethiopian-Israelis — brief

**Recommendation: a short profile (600–800 words, 1–2 charts), not a full page.** No survey gives an Ethiopian-Israeli breakdown on any of the six issues (IDI 2025 had ~7 such respondents). Voting evidence is a few mixed polling stations, 2019–2020.

## Findings that carry the page
1. 177,600 people (end 2024); 47% Israel-born and rising (CBS via JNS/Tedros).
2. No majority town. Highest share: Kiryat Malachi, 13.9% (CBS, end 2023). Largest number: Netanya, 12,900 (CBS, end 2023).
3. Historic lean to Likud. After the 2019 Tekah protests, Likud fell ~11 points and Blue and White gained 9–15 at Ethiopian-neighborhood stations (Ynet). Partly reversed by March 2020.
4. MKs have come from seven parties, Labor to Religious Zionism. On current polls **no Ethiopian-Israeli holds a realistic 2026 slot** (Tamano-Shata #2 on Blue and White, which is below the threshold in every late-August poll — slot unverified; Baruch-Ron around #26–28, Democrats — unverified).
5. Bagrut eligibility is closing fast: 77.7% vs 85.6% in Hebrew education in 2022/23 (CBS); reported at parity in 2023/24 (JPost, unconfirmed). Household income ~20% lower and spending per person ~40% lower (CBS, 2022 survey).
6. Policing is the defining grievance: Tekah killed 2019, officer acquitted 2024. 8–9% of arrests 2019–23 vs 2.2% of Jews (Knesset RIC, secondhand).
7. Enlistment 86% vs 74% (2010; Hebrew Wikipedia, unverified). MK Moshe Solomon broke with Religious Zionism's leadership over the Haredi draft: Smotrich removed him from all Knesset committees in June 2026 after he voted against the Torah-study Basic Law (reports that he left the party are unverified).
