# Brief — Mizrahi-traditional (masorti) Israel

## Findings that carry the page
1. Size depends on the question: 35.8% of Jewish adults (CBS 2026), 31.9% (IDI 2025), 19% as main identity (JPPI 2023).
2. Most masortim are Mizrahi, but most Mizrahim are not masorti: 42% Masorti, about 3 in 10 secular, most of the rest Dati or Haredi (Pew survey 2014–15, published 2016). 15% of Jews say "mixed" origin (IDI 2025).
3. Traditional voters 2022: Likud 46.5%, RZ–Otzma 14%, Yesh Atid 13%, National Unity 12.5%; 57% of Likud voters are traditional (IDI surveys Nov 2022–Oct 2023, published 2026).
4. Six development towns gave the 2022 coalition parties 84% (48% nationally). The shift was inside the right: Likud peaked in 2020 (50.7%), fell to 40.2%; Ben-Gvir–Smotrich rose to 17% (24.6% in Sderot).
5. Supreme Court trust: 21% traditional religious, 40% traditional non-religious, 66% secular (IDI 2025).
6. "Israel ensures citizens' security," 2022→2025: traditional religious 30→51%, secular 52→30%.
7. Draft exemption move "a political deal": 54% traditional non-religious, 37% traditional religious (IDI, Mar 2026).
8. About 2% of Jews name Mizrahi–Ashkenazi as the sharpest tension; 55% name right–left (IDI 2025).
