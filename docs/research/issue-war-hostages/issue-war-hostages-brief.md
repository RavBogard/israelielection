# Brief: the war and the hostages

**Findings**
1. All 251 hostages are home: 166 alive, 85 dead (41 killed in captivity). The last body came back Jan 26, 2026. The open argument now: could a deal have come a year sooner (Nitzan Alon, Oct 3, 2026)?
2. Ceasefire on paper since Oct 10, 2025. In practice Israel held about 70% of Gaza by Aug 2026 (53% at the ceasefire; Chatham House), has struck almost daily (ToI, May 2026), and is stuck on disarm-first vs withdraw-first. The Iran war began Feb 28, 2026; an April 8 ceasefire collapsed July 8 (Wikipedia). Current status needs re-checking. The IDF still holds a zone in south Lebanon.
3. Killed through Oct 4, 2026: 1,318 security personnel and 1,029 civilians. The 70-day reserve cap promised for 2026 is broken (most units 100+ days). The burden falls hardest on religious men: 4.3% serving in an average month, vs 1.7% of secular men and 0.3% of Haredi men.
4. By spring 2025, 52% of right-wing Jews put the hostages first (38.5% toppling Hamas). Jews who wanted the war ended gave the hostages as the reason; 2% cited harm to Gazans.
5. Since the ceasefire the public is doubtful, not dovish. 27% say Israel won in Gaza; 59% think another Oct 7 could happen.
6. INSS (Sept 2026; question format not published): 75% want a state commission, including 52% of coalition voters. Offered a panel without judges, the right picks that. So the real fight is who appoints the panel, which links this issue to the courts. The High Court left the decision to the next government (deadline Jan 15, 2027).
7. Trust among Jews: IDF 73%, Netanyahu 31%, government 25% (July 2026). 68% of Jews say Oct 7 is central to their vote.
