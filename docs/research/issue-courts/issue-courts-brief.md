# Brief: courts and the judicial overhaul (issue-courts)

## Findings that carry the page
1. No constitution: Basic Laws pass by simple majority (1992 dignity law: 32–21); one chamber, no states; the coalition controls the Knesset. Court and attorney general are the main checks.
2. Of the 2023 package only reasonableness passed (July 2023); struck Jan 1, 2024 (8–7), with 12–3 that the court can review Basic Laws.
3. Selection law (late March 2025, 67 votes) takes effect with the next Knesset; 11 justices heard it June 2026, mostly critical; no ruling yet. 4 seats vacant, ~6 more within three years.
4. Court blocked the AG's firing (7–0, Dec 2025) and ruled the Shin Bet chief's firing unlawful (May 2025, split ruling; Sohlberg dissented). AG-split bill: first reading only.
5. Steady majority against: Mar 2026, 52% "not correct" to advance vs 32%. Yet only 18% of Jews call it a main vote factor (Sept 2026).
6. Jewish trust fell before the overhaul (69% 2003 → 41% 2022), flat since (42% 2025). Right 21.5% vs Left 83%; Haredim 3% vs secular 66%.
7. Arabs: 79% opposed the overhaul (Jan 2025), yet 55.5% say the court intervenes too much.
8. Opposition parties propose their own constitution or Basic Law on judicial review, not the old status quo.
