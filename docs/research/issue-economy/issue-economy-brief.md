# Brief: Cost of living and the economy (issue-economy)

## Findings that carry the page
1. **Prices are high, not rising fast.** Food 52% above OECD average (June 2024); inflation ~2%; home prices −1.5 to −2% year on year (CBS mid-2026); new-tenant rent +6.6%.
2. **War bill ~NIS 350bn (2023–26, Bank of Israel).** Debt 60.5% → 68.5% of GDP. Ratings: Moody's Baa1 stable, S&P A stable, Fitch A negative (citing "fractious domestic politics").
3. **2026 budget:** defense up, civilian ministries cut 3%+, Haredi institutions NIS 4.1bn → 5.17bn; passed 62–55 a day before the election trigger.
4. **Haredi men's employment flat at ~53% for a decade** (target 65% by 2030). Arab women 49% and rising.
5. **Non-Haredi Jewish men: 36% of employees, 83% of reserve-duty absences** (Finance Ministry, Aug 2026). The economy and the draft are one fight.
6. **Who is leaving:** 69,300 residents left in 2025 and 19,000 returned (CBS, all residents); high-tech emigration up ~150% since 2022 (Tax Authority).
7. **Economy ties security for Jews** (IDI Sept 2026: 38% vs 39%); first in the Center; second for Arab citizens after crime. In 2022 it led by far (44%, different question).
