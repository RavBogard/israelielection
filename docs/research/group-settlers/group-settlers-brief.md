# Settlers — brief for ruling

## Findings that carry the page
1. **541,085** Jews in West Bank settlements (registry, 1 Jan 2026), about 5.3% of Israel (our arithmetic: 541,085 / ~10.2M); "over 750,000" with East Jerusalem (EU).
2. **About a third are Haredi.** The class's 36/36/28 is Yesha's own May 2023 estimate (ToI, verified), method unstated. Newer counts agree only on Haredim: 33.5% (IPF 2025, share living in Haredi settlements), 34.4% (our sum of the eight Haredi towns in the 2026 registry).
3. **Growth moved:** 2.2% in 2025 vs 1.1% for Israel. Ma'ale Adumim flat for five years; growth is Haredi cities and small ideological communities.
4. **Outposts:** Peace Now's outpost database sheet lists 415 (243 "farms"); counted by founding year, 92 in 2025 and 79 so far in 2026, vs 5–17 a year before 2023. Peace Now's own 2025 summary report says 86 new outposts (60 farms) in 2025. (The sheet's download URL was not recorded; only a saved copy exists.)
5. **Settlers vote in three blocs, not one** (CEC 2022, computed): Haredi cities 90% UTJ+Shas; ideological settlements 57% RZ–Otzma; suburbs 34% Likud.
6. **RZ–Otzma is settler-heavy, not settler-made:** 12.2% of its 2022 vote came from settlements (3.9% of voters), down from 18–21% in 2019–21 as it grew inside the Green Line.
7. **Violence: a few hundred people, rising.** ~300 involved, ~70 hardcore (IDF/Shin Bet); OCHA attacks 1,189 → 1,420 → 1,828 (2023–25); 94% of files closed without indictment (Yesh Din).
8. **No settler-specific opinion survey found.** Attitudes come from religiosity/vote cross-tabs, labeled as such.
