# Israeli TV exit polls against results, 2013-2022

`history.json` holds the exit-poll rows and final seats per election, copied on election night 2026-10-27 from the English Wikipedia polling pages (exit-poll rows) and results pages. `node scripts/live-forecast/exit-history.mjs` derives `summary.json` and the model's `data/exit-poll-calibration.json`.

Method. The Netanyahu-aligned bloc is the one each election's table defined (its Gov./R column). The field is the main channels (Channel 1/Kan 11, Channel 2/12, Channel 10/13) averaged per list, Channel 14 kept as a check, because that average is what the live model computes. Bloc error is final minus field; party error is mean absolute seats per list; a threshold miss is a list shown passing that failed or shown out that passed.

Limits. Wikipedia prints one row per broadcaster and does not separate the 22:00 reading from the corrected version around midnight, so the two cannot be fitted apart; the corrected versions are usually nearer the result (2015, 2021 and 2022 all moved toward it). September 2019 has no exit rows on the English page; it is listed with its result and left out of the fit rather than filled from memory. Two rows are kept as listed although they do not sum to 120 (Channel 1 2015, Channel 12 2021).

Findings (six elections). Field bloc miss mean +0.61 seats, three under and three over, sample sd 2.12, RMS 2.03; per-list MAE 0.76 seats; a wrong threshold call by at least one main channel in four of six (Kadima 2013, Ra'am-Balad and New Right April 2019, Ra'am 2021, Meretz 2022). No channel is consistently better: Kan/Channel 1 mean absolute bloc error 1.5-2.5, Channel 12/2 1.5-2.5, Channel 13/10 1.75-2. Channel 14 has one record (2022), the furthest of four on the bloc (61 against 64). Direction of bias: none that survives shrinkage; the exit polls understate the eventual bloc of the side that ends up stronger more often than they overstate it (2015, April 2019, 2022 under the Netanyahu bloc; 2020 over), which is a compression toward a close result rather than a partisan lean.
