# Brief: the Haredi draft

## Findings that carry the page
1. The 1948 exemption began under a quota; the quota was lifted in 1977. The court struck down each fix (2012, 2017) and in June 2024 ruled 9–0 there is "no legal basis" for not drafting yeshiva students.
2. Enforcement has been slow. In the 2024 draft year, 24,000 orders produced 798 enlistees; ~73,664 men are counted as evaders and 219 have been arrested (court, Sept 2026).
3. Total Haredi enlistment has still roughly doubled: ~1,800 before Oct 7 → ~4,000 in 2025–26 (preliminary), against an annual Haredi cohort of ~14,000 and an IDF target of 4,800. Sources differ on 2024–25: ~2,800 (IDF via ToI) vs ~2,940 (IDI).
4. The reserve burden fell on everyone else: in 2025 religious men 21–44 served at 4.3% a month, secular 1.7%, Haredi 0.3%.
5. Non-Haredi Jewish support for drafting Haredim rose from 67% to 84.5% during 2024; national-religious support doubled. Haredim are the outlier: 61–75% want the status quo.
6. Consensus on "should" splits on "how": JPPI (July 2026) finds 46% for tougher sanctions, 31% only by agreement with Haredi leaders; Likud voters prefer agreement 75%.
7. The draft broke the coalition (UTJ left July 2025; dissolution June–July 2026), but the Knesset was near its full term anyway.
8. Every Zionist opposition party and Amcha make a draft law a coalition condition; Likud has no written position.
