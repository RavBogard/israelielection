# Brief: the West Bank and annexation (issue-west-bank)

## Findings that carry the page
1. **The change happened through administration, not legislation.** The Knesset passed a symbolic 71–13 motion (Jul 2025) and bills 25–24 and 32–9 (Oct 2025; CBS calls the 25–24 vote a preliminary reading, JPost a first reading), but civil powers moved to Smotrich by memorandum (2023) and military order (May 2024), then the Feb 2026 land decisions. Smotrich: "a de facto sovereignty revolution."
2. **Approvals hit a record.** Units advanced: 12,349 (2023) → 9,884 (2024) → 27,941 (2025) → 8,056 to July 29, 2026, and 10,539 to Sept 23, 2026 (both from the same Peace Now page). In 2025, 54 new settlements were approved and 86 outposts set up.
3. **E1 runs into election week.** The tender for 1,234 units closes Oct 19. The AG dropped her postponement on Sept 28.
4. **Settler violence at record levels by OCHA's count:** 1,189 → 1,420 → 1,828 attacks (2023–25). The IDF's own count differs (1,045 → 682 → 867). 93.6% of files closed without indictment (Yesh Din; INSS cites ~94%).
5. **Israelis split three ways, not two.** INSS June 2026: three roughly equal camps of about a quarter each — separation 27%, two states 25%, annexation without civil rights 25% (don't know 10%; about 13% other options). Pew "coexistence possible" fell from 50% (2013) to 17% (2026).
6. **Two states is off the Zionist ballot.** Only Ra'am and the Joint List name it. Yashar calls a Palestinian state "not on the agenda." B'Yachad and Yisrael Beiteinu back sovereignty over Area C.
7. **"Sovereignty" ranges** from all of Judea and Samaria down to Area C, Ma'ale Adumim, or "actions, not words."
