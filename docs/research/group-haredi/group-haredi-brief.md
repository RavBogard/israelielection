# Brief: Haredim (group-haredi)

## Findings that carry the page
1. 1.45M Haredim, 14.3% (IDI 2025; Machon: 1.26M, 12.5%); growth 4.2%/yr; 57% under 20.
2. Projections disagree: CBS 2017 says 32% by 2065; fertility fell 7.5 → 6.1–6.5; Machon's 2065 range is 18–23%; CBS 2050: 24.4%.
3. Lithuanian 34%, Sephardi 33%, Hasidic 29%, Chabad 4%. UTJ runs as one list in 2026 but with three factions; the new one (Homat Torat Yisrael) can split off after the election.
4. In Haredi towns ~9 in 10 votes go to UTJ or Shas, every election 2019–22. Likud 2.6–5%. The far-right list rose from ~2% to ~5.5% once Ben-Gvir joined it (Elad 11%).
5. Women's employment 81% in 2025, about equal to non-Haredi women; men flat at 53–54% for a decade (non-Haredi 87%). Poverty 33–40%.
6. The war moved others on the draft, not Haredim: 90% back freezing arrests of yeshiva students (INSS, July 2026; unverified — no public URL recorded, saved copy agrees). Reserve service 2025: 0.3% of Haredi men in an average month.
7. Supreme Court trust 3%; government 45% (secular 9%); "Israel ensures citizens' security" 68% (secular 30%).
8. Religion-state decides the vote for 41%; security for 5%. Oct 7 central for about a quarter of Haredim vs about three-quarters of secular Jews (IDI, Sept 2026).
