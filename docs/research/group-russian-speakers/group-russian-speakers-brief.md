# Russian-speaking Israelis — brief

## Findings that carry the page
1. **Size depends on who is counted.** About 1.25M people came from the FSU after 1989 (about 13% of the population; Khanin 2023). JPost's 2025 report on the Lazar poll puts eligible FSU-immigrant voters at about 1M (the article's own framing, not a poll result). Only 5.3% of Jews name "FSU immigrant" as their origin (IDI 2025).
2. **About 300–400k are not recognized as Jewish by the Rabbinate** (Khanin; ToI 2019). The non-halachic share of each year's FSU arrivals rose from 6.8% (1990) to 71.7% (2020) (Knesset RIC), so the 2022–25 wave pushes the total higher. No updated total exists. They cannot marry a Jew inside Israel.
3. **The 2022–25 wave was large and is shrinking fast:** 63k FSU arrivals in 2022, 11k in 2025 (Jewish Virtual Library, compiled from CBS/Jewish Agency; unverified — the table could not be read). About 60% of 2014–25 FSU arrivals are considering moving to another country (77% of those aged 18–34) (IDI, Apr 2025).
4. **Yisrael Beiteinu peaked in Sept 2019 and has fallen in every Russian-speaking stronghold since then.** By 2022 it was below its April 2019 level in five of the six strongholds (Haifa is the exception: 8.1% vs 7.3%), yet its national share was above April 2019. The base is shifting from Russian speakers to secular voters generally.
5. **Locality data show no Likud gain.** Evidence of a Likud lean among newcomers is one poll subsample (Lazar: 37% for Likud's Edelstein), and Edelstein may not be on Likud's 2026 list. A second study (Elkayam-Shalem, via EurAsian Times, secondhand) found Russian speakers who arrived after 2012 reported voting 45% opposition vs 13% coalition in 2022 — a different group from Lazar's 2022–25 arrivals, most of whom could not vote in 2022.
6. **Haredi draft is the one consensus:** 94% back a universal draft law (Lazar). 69% do not want Netanyahu to continue as PM; 68% name cost of living as the top concern.
7. **Low turnout:** 51–56% in Bat Yam, Nof HaGalil, Haifa and Kiryat Yam in 2022, against 70.6% nationally.
