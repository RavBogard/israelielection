# Brief: Religion and state

## Findings that carry the page
1. No civil marriage or divorce. The Rabbinate controls Jewish marriage, conversion for marriage, and (since July 15, 2026, again) kashrut. Workarounds: marry abroad (9,550 couples, 2019) or online via Utah (Supreme Court, 2023).
2. The 25th Knesset widened religious authority: rabbinical courts on child support (Nov 2025); rabbinical/Sharia arbitration (Mar 2026, 65–41); Torah study Basic Law (Jul 2026, 63–52); segregated graduate programs (Jul 2026); Western Wall bill advanced 56–47 (Feb 2026). Haredi institutions NIS 4.1bn → 5.17bn (2026 budget).
3. Opinion runs the other way: 59% of Jews back civil marriage (JPPI May 2026). Falls to 46% if intermarriage is named.
4. Shabbat buses outside religious areas: ~74% Jewish support, rising; the right just over half (52%, Mar 2025).
5. Why policy and opinion diverge: religion-state is the most important voting consideration for 67.5% of Haredim vs 15.5% of all Jews (IDI, Aug 2019).
6. Reform 3%, Conservative 2% of Israeli Jews vs 35% / 18% of US Jews (Pew; Israel 2014–15, US 2013).
7. Arab citizens are inside this system too and oppose civil marriage 46–39 (JPPI 2026).
