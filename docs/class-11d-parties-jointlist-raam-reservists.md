## THE JOINT LIST (Hadash–Ta'al–Balad)
A. 2015: formed after threshold rose to 3.25%; all four Arab-led parties; 13 seats (446,583, 10.61%). Apr 2019 split (Hadash–Ta'al / Ra'am–Balad). Sept 2019: 13; 2020: 15 (581,507, 12.67%, peak). 2021: Ra'am left; 6 seats (212,583, 4.82%). 2022: Balad ran alone; Hadash–Ta'al 178,735 (3.75%, 5 seats); Balad 138,617 (2.91%, 0). Jan 22, 2026 preliminary revival agreement (J Street Mar 2026). Aug 19, 2026 formally re-established after collapses over Ta'al's slots (JPost 2026-08-19 article-906019).
B. Yousef Jabareen: Umm al-Fahm (Triangle); constitutional-law scholar; Hadash MK 2015–21; elected Hadash chair May 16, 2026 with 82%, replacing Odeh (chair since 2015); first Hadash leader from the Triangle (ToI 2026-05-16). List (New Arab 2026-09-09): 1 Jabareen (Hadash); 2 Ahmad Tibi (Ta'al); 3 Sami Abu Shehadeh (Balad) — withdrew Oct 1/2; 4 Faten Ghattas (Hadash); 5 Bakr Awawdeh (Balad); 6 Ofer Cassif (Hadash); 7 Youssef Atauna (Hadash); 8 Maha Karkabi-Sabbah. Aug 19 deal: Tibi chairs Knesset faction, becomes deputy speaker, seat on Finance Committee. Replacement at no. 3: not found.
C. 2022 in Arab and Druze localities (IDI/Rudnitzky 2022-12-25): Hadash–Ta'al 28.8%, Balad 21.7%, Ra'am 35.2%; Hadash–Ta'al led the North (31.6%); Triangle three-way split 31–32% each; Arab turnout 53.2% (from 44.6%). Druze votes for Joint List: 11.6% (2015), ~2.9% (Apr 2019), 5.6% (Sept 2019) (Dayan/Brake 2019-11-27). 2026 survey (Dayan/KAP, Rudnitzky, May 2026): expected Arab turnout 53%, 67% "if the Joint List were reestablished as a union of the four Arab parties" (incl. Ra'am — did not happen); 77.2% support an Arab party joining the government (43.3% any coalition, 33.9% center-left); run separately Hadash–Ta'al 5.3 seats, Balad 1.8; four-party list ~16.3. Youth 18–34 expected turnout ~59% (Inter-Agency Task Force 2026-08-12).
D. Goals: "End Gaza destruction and West Bank ethnic cleansing; stop Al-Aqsa incursions and settlement expansion; Palestinian self-determination and statehood" (New Arab 2026-09-09). Jabareen: "push for renewed negotiations with Palestinian leadership to advance the establishment of a Palestinian state alongside Israel" (JPost article-907154). MEF summary adds "a state for all citizens instead of Zionism" and right of return (MEF's words). "Full civic and national equality for Arab citizens" (Hadash principles via israel2026.co.il). Crime: Jabareen calls for "action against organised crime"; Jafar Farah: "There is a failing police commissioner… At this rate this year we will reach 300 murdered" (MEF). 252 Arab citizens killed 2025, record (Abraham Initiatives via JPost 2025-12-29); 147 in first half of 2026, 12% solved (ToI 2026-07-01). No agreed joint platform on draft, judiciary, economy, Netanyahu's trial; hostages statement not found.
E. Slogan: "The only alternative to racism and supremacy." Jabareen: "We agreed on the Joint List because the interests of our people are greater than any disagreement." Aim: remove Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir, Smotrich. CEC Sept 23 (ToI; JPost article-909454): Joint List barred 18–5 (1 abstention; filed by Likud); Cassif 19–5 (Al Jazeera 18–5; Likud); Abu Shehadeh 30–4 (or 31–4; Otzma). AG backed disqualifying only Abu Shehadeh. List's response: "a political, racist, and anti-democratic decision… an attempt to exclude the political representation of more than one million voters." Supreme Court Oct 2, nine justices: list reinstated 9–0; Cassif 7–2 (Mintz, Stein dissenting); evidence lacked "critical mass." On Abu Shehadeh, unanimous that his remarks were "painful, outrageous and nauseating"; President Amit told him at the Oct 1 hearing a majority favored disqualification; he withdrew Thursday night, filed Oct 2, "of my own free will and is irrevocable"; on his Oct 8, 2023 article: "The article contains things that were wrong. I would not say them today" (JPost article-910422). Aug 24: sought EU election monitoring. Jafar Farah (no. 4 in Aug deal) not on filed list after harassment allegations (MEF; reason unconfirmed). Sept 16: "Tayibe unites us" pledge with Ra'am activists.
F. Jabareen's conditions for backing an Eisenkot bloc: principles guaranteeing equality and rights, a development plan, real action on crime, "a clear position against settlements"; opposes any agreement that perpetuates the occupation; didn't rule out "a parliamentary safety net" (INN 2026-08-26; MEF). Won't join Netanyahu. Surplus partner: Ra'am (Sept 11 Ynet / Sept 13 JPost), pledging a campaign "without slander, defamation, incitement, or falsification."
G. Seats: Maariv 7; Ch 13 9; Zman 8; Kan 7; Ch 14 Oct 1: 7. Range 7–9.
H. Kan Sep 28: 55% of Israelis support disqualifying the Joint List. Abu Shehadeh after withdrawing: "a historical catastrophe for democracy, or the very narrow margins of democracy in the State of Israel" (Al Jazeera 2026-10-02).

## RA'AM / UNITED ARAB LIST
A. Founded 1996 as political wing of the Islamic Movement's Southern Branch (accepted Oslo and Knesset participation; Shura Council guided votes). In Joint List 2015–20; left before 2021. June 2, 2021: "first time an independent Arab party became a member of the Israeli government." Dec 7, 2025: Abbas — Ra'am "moving in the direction of being a completely civic party, with separate institutions"; "we are not a part of the Muslim Brotherhood" (ToI). Jan 5, 2026: membership opened to all Israelis. July 26–27, 2026: separation from Shura Council declared complete (MEF).
B. Mansour Abbas b. 1974 Maghar; dentist (HU), MA Haifa, imam; chair since 2018, MK since 2019; "an Arab, a Palestinian, a Muslim, and a citizen of the State of Israel." No. 2 Yoav Segalovitz: Jewish, 67, ex-Lahav 433 commander and Investigations Division head, ex-deputy public security minister; joins the faction, not the party (JPost article-907725; ToI 2026-08-31). Aug 22 slate before Segalovitz: Abbas, Walid Taha, Waleed Alhwashla, Iman Khatib-Yassin, Yasir Hujeirat; final slots 3–6 unconfirmed.
C. 2022: 5 seats, 4.07% (194,047 or 193,916 votes). Seats: 1996 2 · 1999 3 · 2003 1 · 2006 2 · 2009 2 · 2013 3 · Apr 2019 (w/ Balad) 4 · 2021 4 · 2022 5. 2022: 35.2% of Arab/Druze vote; 73.9% in the Negev (IDI). Strongholds: Negev and Triangle. Seeking Christian and Druze voters; 7–8 seat target (Ynet; July 18 launch in Kafr Kanna).
D. Hamas: Kan Reshet Bet July 28, asked whether Hamas is a terror organization to be destroyed: "I don't want to give you a straight answer… I have a straight answer; I just don't want to say it"; called such questions "loyalty tests" (INN 2026-07-28). After Oct 7, 2023: "an inhuman and unjustifiable act." Palestinian state: "The state of Palestine exists and stands, and most of the world recognizes it, except for the US and Israel. We want them to recognize it too" (Aug 22 via MEF). "Israel was born as a Jewish state, and it will remain so" (earlier); AG told CEC the quotes "demonstrate that MK Mansour Abbas recognizes Israel's Jewish character" (ToI 2026-09-23). Crime: "Ra'am chose to return to government with the violence and crime portfolio" (Aug 31); Segalovitz: restore interagency crime programs, civilian service for Arab youth 18–24, Bedouin planning/housing (JPost). Haredi draft, judiciary, trial: not found. Economy: budgets, planning, housing, employment for Arab towns; no full manifesto.
E. Abbas: "The election will determine whether Arab citizens continue with a marginal, protest-based existence… or move toward active participation at the heart of political decision-making" (Ynet). Sept 28 video: "The coalition decides people's lives: policies, laws, budgets." Aug 31: "In every generation there is a moment when fear and hope stand together in the same room. This evening… hope won out" (JTA). CEC Sept 23: barred 18–5 (1 abstention) on petitions from Likud, Otzma, Choosing Life forum; grounds: Abbas's statements, alleged Southern Branch/charity ties; Abbas: "political, improper, and anti-democratic… an attack on Arab-Jewish coexistence." Supreme Court Oct 2: reinstated 9–0. Sept 5: Segalovitz won't be the 61st vote without the party's agreement.
F. Open to any coalition incl. Likud-led (ToI); pitched now as partner for a "change" government. Eisenkot Sept 26: "At the moment I don't see any Arab or Haredi parties who accept these principles, including Mansour Abbas, and therefore he won't be part of my next government." Segalovitz: "Eisenkot won't have a government without Ra'am." Surplus partner: Joint List.
G. Seats: 5 in every poll (Maariv, Ch 13, Zman, Kan; Ch 14 Oct 1: 5).
H. Segalovitz: "I am going to an Arab party. They are not coming to me." ToI survey: 56% of opposition voters back including Ra'am, 30% oppose (ToI 2026-08-31).

## RESERVISTS–ECONOMIC PARTY (ballot letters די)
A. Hendel: Telem/Blue and White 2019; Derekh Eretz with Hauser Mar 2020; communications minister May–Dec 2020 and June 2021–22 (New Hope); no. 2 to Shaked on Zionist Spirit, broke up Sept 2022. The Reservists founded Sept 18, 2025. Alliance with Chili Tropper announced July 8, 2026 ("Yesodot Yisrael" per ToI; "Zionist Home–The Reservists" per JPost); Tropper left for Yashar Sept 6. Same day: partnership with Yaron Zelekha's New Economic Party (0.79% in 2021; 0.29%, 13,920 votes in 2022). List filed Sept 7; technical joint list.
B. Hendel b. 1975 Petah Tikva, raised Elkana; Shayetet 13; reserve lieutenant-colonel; commanded Battalion 8555 in the 2023 war; Netanyahu's communications director Aug 2011–Feb 2012; founded Institute for Zionist Strategies. List (C14 2026-09-07): 1 Hendel; 2 Prof. Yaron Zelekha (b. 1970; accountant-general 2003–07); 3 Einat Wilf (Labor/Independence MK 2010–13; co-author The War of Return; founded Oz Party late 2025; joined Sept 7); 4 Haviv Volibovich; 5 Yoav Adomi.
C. No 2022 seats. Hendel: "Those wanting government with ultra-Orthodox parties know who to vote for in the coalition. Those comfortable with minority government relying on Arab parties have their opposition choice. We must provide the alternative" (Maariv 2026-09-07). Sector data: not found.
D. Universal service: "Those who do not serve cannot vote or be elected to the Knesset"; "Those who give receive; those who don't, don't" (themiluimnikim.org.il); draft law must pass before a government is sworn in (Maariv; JPost article-908761); Zelekha: cap Haredi exemptions at 2,000. Gaza: Hendel earlier proposed moving ~1.5M Gazans to "a tent city outside the Strip" (JPost 2026-08-11 article-904910); Wilf: "Reconstruction you do for a people who actually want to reconstruct"; "full perimeter control," "voluntary emigration directorate for Gaza" (Petek). Palestinian state: "We will not allow a Palestinian state to be established… we support settlement." Judiciary: reform, 8-year term limits, constitution, "Basic Law: Legislation." Oct 7 commission of inquiry: condition. Economy (Zelekha): "Because it's possible to live here differently"; Israel "a paradise for monopolies"; an apartment ≈170 average salaries; against exclusive import deals; earlier: VAT 15%, scrap import protections, abolish agricultural production councils (Bizportal 2021-01-21). Hendel: "Zelekha… comes to break the monopolies making life expensive here, and the Reservists fighting political monopolies." Trial: not found.
E. "Third bloc" message. Pre-merger: "Our mission is solely to establish a Zionist government" (JPost 2026-08-23). First crossed threshold in Ch 13 Sept 23 (n=1,503).
F. Demands: Zionist government only (no Haredi or Arab parties); universal-draft law; state commission; national security ministry for Hendel. Netanyahu: conflicting — "Netanyahu is not my red line" (Aug 11) vs "whoever is responsible for October 7 needs to go home, and that includes Netanyahu" (Aug 23); IsraelEd: would join Netanyahu govt; ToI: non-aligned. Surplus partner: IPF says Yisrael Beiteinu, but YB signed with B'Yachad — unconfirmed.
G. Seats: Maariv 4; Ch 13 below threshold; Zman 5; Kan 4; Ch 14 Oct 1: 1.2% (below). Petek: over threshold in 4 of 10 polls as of Oct 2.
H. "Those who do not serve cannot vote or be elected." Zelekha's 2021 run burned ~₪4M in guarantees (Walla 2021-03-26).
