# Bader-Ofer — slide text and notes (compiled 2026-10-04)
Sources: [S] Shugart, Fruits and Votes, 2026-09-08, https://fruitsandvotes.wordpress.com/2026/09/08/israels-bader-ofer-method-of-allocating-seats-step-by-step/ (2022 tables are images on that page). [K] IDI/Kenig 2026-03-16, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/63750. [R] IDI Shapira & Rahat 2026-04-27, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/64020. [V] IDI "Vote-Sharing Agreements" 2022-10-30, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/47485.

Five steps: (1) discard votes for lists under 3.25% [K]; (2) price of a seat = remaining votes ÷ 120; 2022: 4,347,342 ÷ 120 = 36,227 [S][K]; (3) each list gets a seat per full 36,227 votes; 2022: 113 seats given, 7 left [S][K]; (4) leftovers one at a time: divide each list's votes by (seats + 1), biggest number wins, recalculate, repeat; paired lists count as one [S][V]; (5) inside a pair, the same rule decides which partner gets it [S][K].

2022 worked example [S]: Shas+UTJ 673,158 votes, 17 whole seats, ÷18 = 37,397.67 → wins round 1 (new 35,429.37). National Unity+Yesh Atid 1,279,917, 34 seats, ÷35 = 36,569.06 → round 2 (new 35,553.25). Likud+RZ 1,631,806, 44, ÷45 = 36,262.36 → round 3 (new 35,474.04). Hadash–Ta'al 178,735, 4, ÷5 = 35,747.00 → round 4. Yisrael Beiteinu 213,687, 5, ÷6 = 35,614.50 → round 5. Then NU+YA again (round 6), Likud+RZ again (round 7). Labor 175,992, 4, ÷5 = 35,198.40 and Ra'am 194,047, 5, ÷6 = 32,341.17 get nothing. Inside Likud+RZ: Likud 1,115,336 ÷ 31 = 35,978.58 vs RZ 516,470 ÷ 15 = 34,431.33 → Likud; Likud's new 34,854.25 still beats RZ → Likud takes both, 32 seats instead of 30; RZ stays 14.

Why bigger lists do better: dividing by seats+1 gives a big list more chances to top the table; leftovers lean to larger lists [K][R]; a surplus agreement makes two lists count as one bigger list [R][V]. 2022: under the old largest-remainder rule Likud would have had 31; under Bader-Ofer with RZ's votes, 32; UTJ and Labor each lost a seat they'd have won under the old rule [S]. 2021: the Yesh Atid + Yisrael Beiteinu pair's leftover seat went to the smaller YB; Meretz and New Hope also got their pairs' seats; Likud and Shas as bigger partners got theirs [V]. The catch: an agreement counts only if both pass 3.25%; Labor's 2022 deal with Meretz (150,793 votes) did not count [S][R].

History: 2nd–7th Knessets used largest remainders ("the Hare method"); 1973 the Knesset adopted the method of MKs Yohanan Bader (Gahal) and Avraham Ofer (Alignment), in part "to strengthen the larger parties" [S][V].

For a voter: a vote for a list under 3.25% is thrown out; at the leftover stage a vote for a small unpaired list counts slightly less than one for a big list or a pair. Caveat [R]: apart from leftover seats, "there is almost no electoral advantage to large lists."
