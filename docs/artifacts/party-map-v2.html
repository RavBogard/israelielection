The Party Map

Each block's area is the party's average seat count across four polls (Maariv Oct 2, Channel 13 Oct 1, Zman Yisrael Oct 1, Kan Sep 28). Tap a party for who they are, who votes for them, where they stand, and their seat numbers including Channel 14.

38 lists filed for the Oct 27 election (Central Elections Committee approval, Ynet, Sep 27, 2026). 20+ minor lists are not shown, including Israel First (Sharren Haskel) and the Haredi Public Party (Moti Leitner).
