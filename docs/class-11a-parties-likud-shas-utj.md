# Party profiles: Likud, Shas, UTJ (researched 2026-10-04, Opus subagent)

## LIKUD
A. Founded Sept 13, 1973 by Begin and Sharon (Herut, Liberals, Free Center, National List, Movement for Greater Israel); single party Aug 25, 1988 (Wikipedia Likud; IDI party page https://en.idi.org.il/israeli-elections-and-parties/parties/likud/). 1977: 43 seats (33.4%); "ruling party until 1984." PMs: Begin, Shamir, Netanyahu, Sharon. Netanyahu led 1993–99 and from 2005. Aug 17, 2026 primary: Eli Cohen first; Netanyahu's reserved slots used to move hardliners down; ToI: "win for Netanyahu bid to reshape sinking party" (ToI 2026-08-18, https://www.timesofisrael.com/likud-primary-marks-win-for-netanyahu-bid-to-reshape-sinking-party-and-win-back-voters/).
B. Netanyahu b. Oct 21, 1949 Tel Aviv; Sayeret Matkal 1967–73; UN ambassador 1984–88; PM 1996–99, 2009–21, Dec 29, 2022–; indicted Nov 21, 2019; trial since May 24, 2020 [Wiki-map]. List filed Sept 8 (JPost 2026-09-08 article-907902; i24 2026-09-08): 2 Eli Cohen; 4 Israel Katz; 5 Gideon Sa'ar; 6 Amir Ohana; 7 Yariv Levin; 8 Miri Regev; 9 Talik Gvili (mother of Ran Gvili, last hostage returned); 10 Ofir Katz; 11 Yaakov Bardugo (Channel 14 commentator; opposed inside Netanyahu's circle per Ynet); 15 Elisha Medan (reservist who lost both legs in Gaza); 16 David Peter (attorney; described variously).
C. 2022: 1,115,336 votes, 23.41%, 32 seats. Seats: Apr 2019 35 · Sep 2019 32 · 2020 36 · 2021 30 · 2022 32. Likud voters 2022: 57% traditional, 32% secular, 10% national-religious, 1% Haredi; Likud's share within groups: traditional 46.5%, national-religious 22.5%, secular 20%, Haredi 4% (IDI/Finkelstein 2026-06-28, https://en.idi.org.il/articles/64807, 20 surveys Nov 2022–Oct 2023, n=10,694). Maariv 2022-11-02: "Likud conquered the periphery cities; Yesh Atid leads in Tel Aviv." 2021 city figures: Beersheba 42%, Eilat 42%, Ashkelon 40% (ToI 2021-03-23). First-time voters 2026: Likud 14% vs Yashar 16% (N12/Midgam n=301, ages 18–22, Sept 15–22; JPost 2026-09-23 article-909394). Nimrod Nir: "54.9% of Likud voters favor national unity with centrist parties, versus just 33.9% of party members" (ToI 2026-08-18).
D. No written 2026 platform; Likud "does not participate in questionnaires" (Ynet 2026-09-30, https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h1dpvu9cgl). Draft: Bismuth pushed the exemption bill; Netanyahu revived it as the coalition fell apart; Haskel vowed to fight it (ToI 2026-05-17). Judiciary: Levin on Kan recordings (Aug): "We'll wait for the next term, when two-thirds of the Supreme Court justices' seats become vacant… You'll simply go extinct"; professional members lose veto under new judge-selection law after next government (JTA 2026-09-18, https://www.jta.org/2026/09/18/israel/judicial-reform-2-0-how-netanyahus-plans-to-reshape-the-courts-could-affect-israels-democracy). War/Gaza: Aug 9 rejected Trump's 15-point plan — "Israel rejects the 15-point document"; no withdrawal "until Hamas is genuinely disarmed"; "as long as I am prime minister, no Palestinian state will arise" (Al Jazeera 2026-08-09). Ran Gvili's body returned Jan 26, 2026: "We promised… to bring everyone back… to the very last one" (ToI). West Bank: July 2025 all Likud ministers urged "apply sovereignty"; no documented response (Ynet 2025-07-02). Religion-state 2026: not found. Economy: JPost review of 2022 promises — Israel still among five most expensive OECD countries; free childcare 0–3 not reached; Arab-sector murders 116→252 (JPost 2026-07-26 article-903632). Trial/pardon: pardon request Nov 30, 2025 ("immediate conclusion of the trial would greatly help to lower the flames"); Herzog deferred Apr 26, 2026, pushing a plea deal; Netanyahu "has not yet responded" as of Sept 19 (JPost 2026-09-19 article-909097). Arab parties: Likud petitioned to bar Joint List for supporting "armed struggle"; CEC voted 18–5 to bar Joint List and Ra'am with Likud, Shas, UTJ reps in favor (ToI 2026-09-23); Supreme Court reversed Oct 2 (Al Jazeera 2026-10-02). Ohana: "Eisenkot has no coalition without Abbas" (Arab Center DC 2026-10-02).
E. Slogan: "Netanyahu does not surrender" / "לא נכנע" (Reuters via JPost 2026-09-29 article-910068; Ynet). Netanyahu: "There is no straight. Yashar is a bluff. There's either left or right… Do you want a Palestinian state? (or) do you want a Jewish state?" (JPost/Reuters 2026-09-29). AI image/video of Eisenkot embracing Mansour Abbas (same; Arab Center DC). UNGA speech Sept 24. Aug ad placing Mamdani with Iran/Hezbollah leaders (Haaretz 2026-08-16 headline). Ads mocking Eisenkot's English (Ynet). Likud court chair Kleiner: surplus deal with RZ "will cost half a seat" (Kikar 2026-09-18).
F. "a strong right-wing government led by me – or a weak l[eft]…" (JPost 2026-09-08, truncated). Surplus partner: Religious Zionism — agreed Sept 8 (Israel Hayom), unsigned as of Sept 15 (Channel 14); final status not found.
G. Seats: Maariv Oct 2: 19; Ch 13 Oct 1: 19; Zman Oct 1: 21; Kan Sep 28: 21; Ch 14 Sep 28: 31 (Sep 23: 32; Oct 1: 32). Range 19–32.
H. Talik Gvili at no. 9; Ran, a police officer killed Oct 7, was the last hostage returned after 843 days: "The first to go out, the last to come back. Our hero." (ToI 2026-01-26; i24 2026-09-08). Levin: "You'll simply go extinct" (JTA 2026-09-18).

## SHAS
A. Founded 1984 as "Shomrei Sefarad" out of protest at Agudat Yisrael's thin Sephardi representation; Rav Ovadia Yosef's Council of Torah Sages; Rav Shach co-patron until 1990; "returning the crown to its former glory" (IDI party page https://en.idi.org.il/israeli-elections-and-parties/parties/shas/). Leaders: Deri 1990–99, Yishai 1999–2012, Deri from 2013. Peak 17 seats (13.0%) 1999; 7 in 2015 after Rav Ovadia's death Oct 2013. 2026: Council head Rabbi Moshe Maya chose former Chief Rabbi Yitzhak Yosef "to lead" (JPost 2026-08-20 article-906132).
B. Deri b. Feb 17, 1959 Meknes; immigrated 1968; interior minister 1988 (age 27 or 29 — sources conflict); bribery conviction 1999, 22 months; back as chair 2013; tax plea Jan 2022; Supreme Court ruled him unfit Jan 18, 2023, dismissed Jan 24 [Wiki-map]. List Sept 8 (JPost article-907976): 1 Deri; 2 Yinon Azoulay; 3 Michael Malkieli; 4 Yoav Ben-Tzur; 5 Chaim Biton; 6 Lt.-Col. (res.) Dror Amos (Haifa religious council head; 25 yrs IDF; founded "Kaddish for Every Holy Soul" after Oct 7); 7 Moshe Abutbul; 11 Yosef Alnathanov (Bukharan community). ITIM petitioned to disqualify Amos over cooling-off period (INN 2026-09-15); outcome not found.
C. 2022: 392,964 votes, 8.25%, 11 seats. Seats: Apr 2019 8 · Sep 2019 9 · 2020 9 · 2021 9 · 2022 11. 2022 shares: 28% of Haredim, 10.5% of national-religious (IDI 2026-06-28). Malach (IDI): "Shas has at least three or four Knesset seats' worth of voters who are simply not Haredi" (Shomrim 2026-06-30). Contested-voter places: southern moshavim, Kiryat Shmona, Shlomi, Or Yehuda (ToI 2026-08-21, https://www.timesofisrael.com/eisenkot-threatens-to-siphon-traditional-voters-away-from-a-panicking-shas/). First-time voters: 3% overall, 20% of Haredi first-timers (N12 via JPost 2026-09-23).
D. Draft: Deri Sept 3: "The army doesn't want haredi soldiers, get that into your head" (JPost article-907424); told Netanyahu that without two laws protecting yeshiva students "there was nothing to discuss" (ToI 2026-07-12, reported speech); bill enshrining Torah study as a basic value (JPost 2026-09-08). Judiciary: "fundamentally reform the judicial system and return power to the people and their elected representatives" (Ynet hkcjtk1cme). Gaza: supports dismantling Hamas, opposes indefinite Israeli control (JPost characterization 2026-09-08). West Bank: opposes Palestinian state; "not central" (Ynet 2026-09-30). Religion-state: Orthodox control of marriage, conversion, kashrut; opposes civil marriage and expanded non-Orthodox Kotel prayer (JPost 2026-09-08). Economy: Deri Dec 9, 2025: "It is unacceptable that a poor Haredi child would not receive the minimum support as a poor Arab child does" (ToI). Pardon: not found. Arab parties: voted to bar (ToI 2026-09-23).
E. Ad with IDF soldier pulled on interim order from Justice Sohlberg (JC 2026-08-24): caption "more than 30,000 conscripts and career soldiers voted for Shas"; tagline "Faith, tradition, unity, and love of Israel" (JPost article-906299); Eisenkot: "You cannot promote draft evasion… and then use IDF soldiers as backdrop." Rabbi Yitzhak Yosef, July: "Will Bibi Netanyahu repent? Not a chance… Eisenkot, perhaps he will" (ToI 2026-07-12); Deri July 16: "We support Netanyahu, period"; Yosef Sept 10 turned on Eisenkot over mixed-gender tank crews while still calling Netanyahu "a liar" (INN). Slogan: none found.
F. "We support Netanyahu." Reported plan to sit in opposition until a draft law passes — anonymous, "not a formal decision" (Maariv via JPost 2026-09-17 article-908924). Surplus partner: UTJ "expected" (JPost 2026-09-10); 2026 signing not found.
G. Seats: Maariv 7; Ch 13 7; Zman 9; Kan 7; Ch 14 10. Range 7–10.
H. The pulled soldier ad. A reserve lieutenant colonel at no. 6 the same month Deri said "The army doesn't want haredi soldiers."

## UNITED TORAH JUDAISM
A. Joint list of Agudat Yisrael (Hasidic) and Degel HaTorah (Lithuanian) since 1992 (IDI party page). Degel founded by Rav Shach breaking from Agudah. Split Jan 2004, reunited Feb 2006. Members served only as deputy ministers until recently. July 2025: both factions quit the government over the draft. Sept 2026 reshuffle: Sept 5 Rav Dov Landau removed Gafni and Maklev from the slate by letter before Shabbat; Yaakov Asher made head (Yeshiva World 2026-09-05). Sept 8: Meir Porush and Elyakim Stark formed "Homat Torat Yisrael" inside the list — first third component since 1992 (Davar 2026-09-08, https://www.davar1.co.il/696518/); trigger: Shlomei Emunim and Belz said Gur delayed fund transfers (INN 2026-09-08). Degel's rabbis: Dov Landau, Moshe Hillel Hirsch.
B. Yaakov Asher (Degel) b. July 2, 1965 Ramat Gan; Bnei Brak mayor from 2008; MK since 2013; Deputy Speaker 2022; resigned Interior Committee chair over draft (Ynet 2026-09-06). Asher: "Whoever wants comfortable government partners will need to sit with us." List Sept 8 (Davar; Israel Hayom): 1 Asher (Degel); 2 Goldknopf (Agudah/Gur); 3 Pindrus (Degel); 4 Porush (Homat); 5 Rosenthal (Degel); 6 Stark (Homat); 7 Weisfish (Degel); 8 Tessler (Agudah). UTJ LIST HEAD NOW SETTLED: Asher.
C. 2022: 280,194 votes, 5.88%, 7 seats. Seats: Apr 2019 8 · then 7, 7, 7, 7. 58% of Haredim voted UTJ in 2022; 86% UTJ or Shas (IDI 2026-06-28). UTJ voters 3.3% of population in 1992 → 5.9% (INSS 2022-12-01). 2021: Modi'in Illit 78%, Bnei Brak 60.9% (ToI). First-time voters: 9% overall; 45% of Haredi first-timers (N12). Chabad-affiliated poll: 40% would prefer a joint Ben-Gvir–Smotrich slate (Shomrim 2026-06-30).
D. Draft: Asher Sept 28: "United Torah Judaism, with all its components, will not support or enter any government without regulating the status of Torah learners" (Matzav). Rav Landau letter May 2026: "We no longer have trust in Netanyahu" (JPost 2026-05-25 article-897241). Judiciary: "The Supreme Court has accumulated excessive power" (Ynet). West Bank: no unified policy; Agudah leans right, Degel "more moderate" (Ynet 2026-09-30). Gaza: "focuses primarily on domestic issues" (JPost 2026-09-08). Religion-state: as Shas. Economy: housing for Haredi communities, yeshiva funding. Pardon: not found. Arab parties: voted to bar.
E. Degel slogan "הזכות להיות חרדי" / "The right to be Haredi," launched Sept 28 (JDN). Gafni: acts by "Torah guidance, whether right is left or left is right." Rabbinic call "do all that they instruct you" with recorded calls from each court's leader (Ynet). Asher on RZP/Otzma courting Haredim: "Many things look like etrogim – but this isn't an etrog" (INN 2026-09-27). Channel 12: Goldknopf and Tessler might abstain to let an Eisenkot minority government form; Asher denied: "no desire to topple or install anyone" (ToI 2026-09-29).
F. No government without a yeshiva-status law, whoever leads (Asher Sept 28). Porush's faction requires separate negotiations (INN 2026-09-08). Surplus partner: Shas "expected"; signing not found.
G. Seats: Maariv 7; Ch 13 8; Zman 8; Kan 7; Ch 14 8. Range 7–8.
H. First time since 1992 the "two-party" Haredi list runs as three. Gafni, one of the longest-serving MKs, removed from the slate by a letter from his rabbi on Friday before Shabbat; stays party chairman.
