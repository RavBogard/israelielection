## Size, growth and geography

At the end of 2024, 177,600 Israelis were of Ethiopian origin ([CBS, reported by JNS, November 2025](https://www.jns.org/israels-jewish-ethiopian-population-reached-177600-in-2024/)). The Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) counts people born in Ethiopia and Israeli-born children of Ethiopian-born fathers. A growing share were born in Israel.

<Chart id="ethiopian-israelis.population" />

No town has an Ethiopian-Israeli majority. The highest share is in Kiryat Malachi, 13.9% of residents; the largest numbers are in Netanya (12,900), Beersheba (10,600) and Petah Tikva (10,100), all at the end of 2023 ([CBS, 2024](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/mediarelease/DocLib/2024/371/11_24_371b.pdf)). Between 1,680 and 1,812 people a year came from Ethiopia in 2021–23 (same source). More than 7,000 people of Ethiopian Jewish descent were still waiting in Gondar and Addis Ababa in September 2025, many with close family in Israel; some are labeled Beta Israel and others Falash Mura ([Shmuel Legesse, Jerusalem Post op-ed, September 2025](https://www.jpost.com/opinion/article-867319)).

Gaps in schooling and income remain. In 2022/23, 77.7% of Ethiopian-origin 12th-graders were eligible for a matriculation (bagrut) certificate, against 85.6% in Hebrew-language education. In the 2022 household survey, net household income was about 20% below the general population (NIS 14,676 against 18,237 a month) and spending per person about 40% lower ([CBS, 2024](https://www.cbs.gov.il/he/mediarelease/DocLib/2024/371/11_24_371b.pdf)).

## How their towns have voted

Results are reported by place, and no place is mostly Ethiopian-Israeli. The closest evidence is a press analysis of polling stations in Ethiopian-Israeli neighborhoods before and after the 2019 protests over the Tekah shooting (below). It shows how these polling stations voted, not how Ethiopian-Israelis voted.

<Chart id="ethiopian-israelis.stations-2019" />

Likud fell 10 to 11 points at each station and Blue and White gained 9 to 15 ([Ynet, September 2019](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-5591507,00.html)). By March 2020 the shift had partly reversed: Blue and White fell to 18.5% at Ramat Eliyahu stations and 20.1% in Rehovot, and a community activist said many people he knew had gone back to Likud ([Ynet, March 2020](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-5688315,00.html)). Kiryat Malachi, the town with the largest Ethiopian-Israeli share, moved the same way.

<Chart id="ethiopian-israelis.kiryat-malachi" />

This page stops at 2020. No station-level analysis was found for 2021 or 2022, and whole-town results say little about a group that is at most a seventh of any town's residents.

## What they think on the six issues

No survey breaks out Ethiopian-Israelis on the Haredi draft, the courts, the war and the hostages, the West Bank, religion and state, or the economy, in 2026 or earlier. The samples are too small: in the Israel Democracy Institute's 2025 Democracy Index, 0.6% of the 1,205-person Jewish sample gave Ethiopian as their ethnicity, about seven people ([IDI, 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/media/30427/the-israeli-democracy-index-2025-full-english-version.pdf)). The income figures above are the nearest measure on the economy. In a 2012 survey of immigrants who arrived from 1979 to 1991, almost all said they felt at home in Israel, and about a third reported personal discrimination at work and in the army ([Myers-JDC-Brookdale, 2012, the only such survey found](https://brookdale.jdc.org.il/en/publication/twenty-years-later-survey-ethiopian-immigrants-lived-israel-two-decades/)).

## Who they are

Policing has been at the center of the community's protests. In 2019 a police officer's warning shot at the ground ricocheted and killed Solomon Tekah, 18. The officer was acquitted by the Haifa Magistrate's Court on April 2, 2024, on grounds of self-defense ([Times of Israel, April 2024](https://www.timesofisrael.com/police-officer-in-solomon-tekah-slaying-acquitted-of-negligent-manslaughter/)). "We had a small hope and it was dashed. There is no justice," his mother said. Police internal investigators appealed in May 2024 ([Times of Israel, May 2024](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/police-investigators-appeal-acquittal-of-officer-in-killing-of-ethiopian-israeli-teen/)); no outcome was found.

When the community arrived in the 1980s, the Rabbinate required a symbolic renewal of their Jewish identity by ritual immersion, which no one argued was a conversion; many in the community saw it as humiliating and unnecessary, and the practice ended in 1985 after a month-long strike ([Atira Winchester, My Jewish Learning](https://www.myjewishlearning.com/article/ethiopian-jews-in-israel/)).

Older voters have leaned to Likud; younger voters, focused on how police treat them, have been readier to move ([Times of Israel, March 2020](https://www.timesofisrael.com/in-southern-likud-stronghold-young-ethiopians-lose-patience-with-status-quo/)).
