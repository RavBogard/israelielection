## What is at stake

The Gaza war (officially in Israel, "Swords of Iron") began with the Hamas-led attack of October 7, 2023. Three years on, the rescue question is closed. All 251 people abducted that day have come home: 166 alive and 85 dead, of whom 41 were taken alive and killed in captivity ([Times of Israel, Oct 3, 2026](https://timesofisrael.com/ex-hostage-point-man-says-israel-could-have-returned-all-oct-7-captives-a-year-earlier)). The last body, that of police officer Ran Gvili, came back on January 26, 2026 ([Times of Israel, Jan 26, 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/all-of-them-home-now-body-of-last-hostage-ran-gvili-found-in-gaza-returned-to-israel/)). The live arguments are whether they could have come home sooner, and who investigates Oct 7. Nitzan Alon, the IDF's former hostage point man, said in October 2026 that Israel "could and should have shortened the war" and brought the captives home "maybe a year earlier," and that Netanyahu "also had his political and personal considerations."

**Gaza.** A ceasefire under a US-brokered deal took effect on October 10, 2025 ([Times of Israel, Oct 10, 2025](https://www.timesofisrael.com/gaza-ceasefire-starts-as-idf-completes-partial-withdrawal-hamas-has-72-hours-to-return-hostages/)). The share of Gaza that Israel occupies grew from about 53% at the ceasefire to nearly 70% by August 2026 ([Chatham House, Aug 3, 2026](https://www.chathamhouse.org/2026/08/agreeing-disarm-hamas-trying-call-israels-bluff-gaza)), and Israel has carried out "near-daily strikes throughout Gaza since the ceasefire" ([Times of Israel, May 5, 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/board-of-peace-wont-hold-israel-to-truce-terms-if-hamas-doesnt-okay-disarmament-offer/)). As of August 2026 the sides were stuck on sequence: Israel wants Hamas disarmed before it withdraws; Hamas wants Israel out first (Chatham House). The Gaza Health Ministry reports 73,922 Palestinians killed since October 2023, as of September 23, 2026 ([UN OCHA, Sept 23, 2026](https://www.ochaopt.org/content/reported-impact-snapshot-gaza-strip-23-september-2026)), including 1,402 since the ceasefire was announced ([UN OCHA situation report, Sept 25, 2026](https://www.ochaopt.org/content/humanitarian-situation-report-25-september-2026)).

**Iran and Lebanon.** The US-Israel war on Iran began on February 28, 2026 ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/1/us-military-says-launching-new-attacks-on-iran)). A two-week US–Iran ceasefire was announced on April 8 ([Al Jazeera, Apr 8, 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/8/us-iran-ceasefire-deal-what-are-the-terms-and-whats-next)). A June 17 memorandum broke down within a month amid renewed US strikes and expired on August 17 with no deal ([Al Jazeera, Aug 17, 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/17/us-iran-memorandum-of-understanding-expires-how-and-why-it-fell-apart)). On September 1 the US struck Iranian military sites and Iran fired at US bases, with diplomacy "at an impasse" ([Al Jazeera, Sept 1, 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/1/us-military-says-launching-new-attacks-on-iran)). In Lebanon, Netanyahu has said forces would hold a "security zone" as long as "Israel's security needs require it" ([PBS/AP, June 19, 2026](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/israel-and-hezbollah-renew-ceasefire-after-u-s-and-iran-call-off-talks-over-fighting-in-lebanon)), and troops were still there in late September ([Times of Israel, Sept 28, 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-28-2026/)).

**The cost at home.** From Oct 7, 2023 to Oct 4, 2026, 1,318 Israeli security personnel and 1,029 civilians were killed, by Defense Ministry and National Insurance Institute counts ([Arutz Sheva, Oct 4, 2026](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/434051)). The IDF promised to cap reserve duty at 70 days in 2026; by late September "most reserve units have already exceeded 100 days" ([Jerusalem Post, Sept 25, 2026](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-909671)). The load is uneven: in an average month of 2025, 4.3% of religious Jewish men aged 21–44 were on reserve duty, against 1.7% of secular men and 0.3% of Haredi (ultra-Orthodox) men ([IDI, Aug 20, 2026](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/65427)). See [the draft page](/issues/haredi-draft).

**The inquiry.** A state commission of inquiry under the 1968 law has its members appointed by the Supreme Court president and must be headed by a judge ([IDI, Dec 24, 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/62785)). Netanyahu opposes one. His coalition's alternative, described as a "national" commission, has no judges choosing its members: 80 Knesset members would appoint it, or else coalition and opposition would name three each. It passed a first reading 59–0 on July 6, 2026, with the opposition boycotting ([Times of Israel, July 7, 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/knesset-passes-controversial-politically-appointed-oct-7-probe-in-1st-reading/)), and was not completed before the end of the Knesset term ([Ynet, Oct 4, 2026](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/r1sz8wyimx)). On September 23 the Supreme Court left the decision to the next government, which must respond by January 15, 2027 ([Times of Israel, Sept 23, 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/high-court-delays-decision-on-october-7-state-commission-until-after-election/)).

## What Israelis think

No published 2026 survey breaks these questions down by religiosity; the figures below are by political camp, or Jews and Arabs.

During the war the public put the hostages ahead of toppling Hamas, and by spring 2025 so did a narrow majority of the Jewish right.

<Chart id="war-hostages.hostages-first" />

In September 2025, among Jews who wanted the war ended, 50.5% gave the hostages' danger as the main reason and 2% gave reducing harm to Gaza's residents ([IDI, Sept 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/61845)).

Since the ceasefire the mood is doubtful rather than dovish. Only 27% say Israel won in Gaza, and 59% think an attack like Oct 7 could come again from Gaza, up from 52% a year earlier ([INSS, Sept 24, 2026](https://www.inss.org.il/publication/three-years-survey/)). The war also sits near the center of the vote: 68% of Jewish Israelis and 32% of Arab Israelis call Oct 7 a central factor in choosing a party ([IDI, Aug 31–Sept 3, 2026](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/66177)).

On the inquiry, the headline number depends on the question. Asked to support or oppose a state commission, 74% backed one in September 2025 ([IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/61845)); INSS found 75% support in September 2026, including 52% of coalition voters ([INSS, Sept 24, 2026](https://www.inss.org.il/publication/three-years-survey/)). Offered a commission without judges as well, many on the right choose that instead.

## What the parties say

On Gaza, Netanyahu said in August that "Israel rejects the 15-point document" of the Board of Peace and that the army "will not carry out any withdrawal until Hamas is genuinely disarmed" ([Al Jazeera, Aug 9, 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/israel-rejects-trumps-15-point-plan-for-gaza)). Criticizing the plan, opposition leader Naftali Bennett said Israel "must not budge a centimeter from the red line until Hamas is disarmed and the weapons are removed from Gaza," and Gadi Eisenkot said Israel "must not accept a reality in which Hamas survives, rearms, and waits for the opportunity to carry out the next massacre" ([Jerusalem Post, Aug 1, 2026](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-904244)). Itamar Ben-Gvir's "Disengagement 710" plan targets 250,000 Gazans leaving in its first year ([Jerusalem Post, Sept 3, 2026](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-907523)), and Ofer Winter launched his party saying "There is one solution in Gaza: migration" ([Ynet, Aug 25, 2026](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/r1xkosjpzl)).

On the inquiry, Ynet asked every party the same question in October 2026; most coalition parties did not answer.

## Misreadings

**"The Israeli argument is the American one."** An American reader may expect the argument to center on Palestinian civilians. In Israel the mainstream argument was hostages against fighting on, and now it is accountability. In July 2025, 78% of Jews said Israel is "making substantial efforts to avoid causing unnecessary suffering to Palestinians in Gaza"; 22.5% of Arab citizens agreed ([IDI, July 2025](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/60357)). Views closer to American critics' are held mainly by [Palestinian citizens of Israel](/communities/palestinian-citizens), 93% of whom wanted the war ended in September 2025 ([IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/61845)).

**"Anti-Netanyahu means dovish."** Bennett and Eisenkot, who both promise a state commission, criticized the Board of Peace plan over Hamas's disarmament.

**"The war is over."** Gaza has a ceasefire, but Israel held nearly 70% of the Strip in August 2026 and struck it near-daily, and troops stayed in southern Lebanon into late September.

**"The hostage families were one movement."** In June 2025 the Hostages and Missing Families Forum called "the return of all hostages through a comprehensive deal" "the victory of this war" ([Times of Israel, June 22, 2025](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/hostage-forum-welcomes-return-of-slain-captives-urges-government-to-use-iran-momentum-for-deal-to-free-all-hostages/)). After six hostages were found killed, organizers estimated 500,000 people rallied on September 1, 2024 ([Times of Israel, Sept 1, 2024](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-september-1-2024/)). The Tikva Forum, another group of hostage families, seeks every captive's release "by means which strengthen the State of Israel," and says "our only option is to win this war" ([Tikva Forum](https://tikva-leumit.org.il/en/eng/)). Its former leader Zvika Mor, father of ex-hostage Eitan Mor, joined Religious Zionism in May 2026 ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/topic/tikva-forum/)).

**"The inquiry is a side issue."** It is also a fight about judges: whether the Supreme Court president picks the panel. See [courts and the judicial overhaul](/issues/courts).
